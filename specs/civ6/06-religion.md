# 06 Religion

Reference data: beliefs, religions and religious units in [data/religion.md](data/religion.md); worship buildings in [data/buildings.md](data/buildings.md); constants (RELIGION_*, COMBAT_RELIGIOUS_*) in [data/global-parameters.md](data/global-parameters.md).

## Faith
Faith is a yield accumulated at empire level. Sources: Holy Site and its buildings, pantheon/religion beliefs, natural wonders, policies, city-states (Religious), Stonehenge etc. Spent on: pantheon founding, religious units, worship buildings (bought only with Faith), Great People patronage, Theocracy military purchases, certain civ abilities, Rock Bands [GS], Naturalists, builders/settlers with the Monumentality dedication [R&F], Soothsayers [mode].

## Pantheon
- Available once a player has accumulated **25 Faith** (data: GlobalParameters.RELIGION_PANTHEON_MIN_FAITH; whether this is scaled by game speed: engine; unverified). A blocking choice: pick one pantheon belief not already taken by another player.
- Pantheon beliefs in the Gathering Storm ruleset (23; full effects in [data/religion.md](data/religion.md#pantheon)): City Patron Goddess (+25% district production in cities with no specialty district), Dance of the Aurora (Holy Site +1 Faith per adjacent Tundra), Desert Folklore (Holy Site +1 Faith per adjacent Desert), Divine Spark (+1 Great Prophet point from Holy Sites, +1 Great Scientist from Libraries, +1 Great Writer from Amphitheaters), Earth Goddess (+1 Faith on tiles with Breathtaking appeal, i.e. 4+), Fertility Rites (+10% growth and a free Builder), God of Craftsmen (+1 Production and +1 Faith on improved strategic resources), God of Healing (+30 healing for units adjacent to a Holy Site), God of the Forge (+25% production toward Ancient and Classical military units), God of the Open Sky (+1 Culture per Pasture), God of the Sea (+1 Production per Fishing Boats), God of War (Faith equal to 50% of a killed unit's strength when it dies within 8 tiles of a Holy Site), Goddess of Festivals (+1 Culture per Plantation), Goddess of Fire [GS] (+2 Faith on geothermal fissures and volcanic soil), Goddess of the Hunt (+1 Food and +1 Production per Camp), Initiation Rites (+50 Faith per barbarian outpost cleared, and the clearing unit heals 100 HP), Lady of the Reeds and Marshes (+2 Production on Marsh, Oasis and desert floodplains), Monument to the Gods (+15% production toward Ancient and Classical wonders), Religious Idols (+2 Faith on mines over luxury and bonus resources), Religious Settlements (+15% border growth and a free Settler), River Goddess (+2 Amenities and +2 Housing in cities with a Holy Site next to a river), Sacred Path (Holy Site +1 Faith per adjacent Rainforest), Stone Circles (+2 Faith per Quarry).
- Pantheon effects mostly apply to cities that follow your pantheon (or your religion once founded; the pantheon becomes part of it). A few apply to the whole player (God of Healing, God of the Forge, God of War, Initiation Rites, Monument to the Gods, the free units of Fertility Rites and Religious Settlements). Exact follower semantics for pantheons after another religion takes over a city: engine; unverified.

## Founding a religion
- Maximum religions per game is set by map size, not by the actual number of players (data: Map_GreatPersonClasses.MaxWorldInstances for Great Prophets): Duel 2, Tiny 3, Small 4, Standard 5, Large 6, Huge 7. Adding extra players does not raise the limit (engine; source: https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Religion_(Civ6)). Once all are claimed, no more Great Prophets can be earned. Each player can earn at most one Great Prophet (data: GreatPersonClasses.MaxPlayerInstances = 1).
- Requires a **Great Prophet** (GPP from Holy Sites; Stonehenge grants one) activated on a completed Holy Site (or a unique replacement such as the Lavra; data: Districts.AllowsHolyCity) or on Stonehenge. That city becomes the **Holy City**.
- Choose name/icon from the list (Buddhism, Catholicism, Confucianism, Eastern Orthodoxy, Hinduism, Islam, Judaism, Protestantism, Shinto, Sikhism, Taoism, Zoroastrianism; or custom).
- On founding, choose **1 Founder belief** and **1 Follower belief** (data: RELIGION_INITIAL_BELIEFS = 2). The religion is then **enhanced** by Apostles: each use of an Apostle's **Evangelize Belief** (consumes the Apostle) adds one belief from a class not yet filled. Each class holds at most one belief (data: BeliefClasses.MaxInReligion), so the final religion is pantheon + Founder + Follower + Worship + Enhancer. Beliefs are unique per world.

### Belief categories (Gathering Storm list)
- **Founder** (benefits only the founder, scaled by spread): Cross-Cultural Dialogue (+1 Science per 4 followers), Lay Ministry (+1 Faith per Holy Site and +1 Culture per Theater Square in following cities), Papal Primacy (sending an envoy to a city-state adds 200 pressure of your religion there), Pilgrimage (+2 Faith per following city), Religious Unity (+1 envoy when a city-state first adopts your religion), Sacred Places (+2 Science, Culture, Gold and Faith per following city with a world wonder), Stewardship (+1 Science per Campus and +1 Gold per Commercial Hub in following cities), Tithe (+3 Gold per following city), World Church (+1 Culture per 4 followers). (Church Property is not in this ruleset.)
- **Follower** (applies to each following city): Choral Music (Shrines and Temples give Culture equal to their base Faith), Divine Inspiration (+4 Faith per world wonder), Feed the World (Shrines and Temples +3 Food and +2 Housing), Jesuit Education (buy Campus and Theater Square buildings with Faith), Religious Community (international trade routes +2 Gold for a Holy Site plus +2 per Holy Site building), Reliquaries (relics give triple Faith and Tourism), Warrior Monks (buy Warrior Monks with Faith; culture bomb when a Holy Site is completed), Work Ethic (Holy Site Faith adjacency also gives Production), Zen Meditation (+1 Amenity in cities with 2+ specialty districts).
- **Worship** (unlocks one Holy Site building, bought with Faith, cost 190, requires a Temple; each gives +3 Faith unless stated; data: Buildings): Cathedral (1 religious art slot), Dar-e Mehr (+1 Faith per era since built or repaired), Gurdwara (+2 Food, +1 Housing), Meeting House (+2 Production), Mosque (religious units bought there get +1 spread charge), Pagoda (+1 Diplomatic Favor per turn [GS]), Stupa (+1 Amenity), Synagogue (+5 Faith instead of +3), Wat (+2 Science).
- **Enhancer**: Crusade (+10 CS for combat units near foreign cities following your religion), Defender of the Faith (+5 CS for combat units in friendly territory of cities following your religion), Holy Order (Missionaries and Apostles 30% cheaper), Holy Waters (+10 healing for your religious units in or next to Holy Sites of cities following your religion), Itinerant Preachers (passive pressure range +3 tiles, i.e. 10 → 13), Missionary Zeal (religious units ignore terrain and feature movement costs), Monastic Isolation (your religion's pressure never drops from theological combat losses), Religious Colonization (cities you found start with your majority religion), Scripture (+25% passive pressure from nearby cities).

## Spread mechanics
- **Followers**: each city tracks accumulated pressure per religion. Followers of a religion = city population × that religion's pressure ÷ total pressure, rounded (engine; source: https://www.epochalmanac.com/civ-6/victory/religious-victory/). The **majority religion** is the one followed by more than half of the citizens; if none does, the city has no majority religion.
- **Passive pressure**: every city with a majority religion adds pressure each turn to every city within **10 tiles** (RELIGION_SPREAD_ADJACENT_CITY_DISTANCE): base 1 (RELIGION_SPREAD_ADJACENT_PER_TURN_PRESSURE), ×2 if the source city has a Holy Site (RELIGION_SPREAD_HOLY_SITE_PRESSURE_MULTIPLIER), ×4 if it is the Holy City (RELIGION_SPREAD_HOLY_CITY_PRESSURE_MULTIPLIER). The two multipliers stack, so a Holy City with a Holy Site gives ×8 (engine; source: epochalmanac link above). Beliefs and civ abilities modify range and amount (Itinerant Preachers, Scripture).
- **Trade routes** carry pressure: the origin's religion is pushed into the destination at factor 1.0, and the destination's into the origin at 0.5 (data: RELIGION_SPREAD_TRADE_ROUTE_PRESSURE_FOR_DESTINATION / _FOR_ORIGIN; exact per-turn amount: engine; unverified).
- New population: each point of population carries 50 base pressure (data: RELIGION_SPREAD_ATHEISM_PRESSURE_PER_POP; the epochalmanac guide reports it as +50 to the majority religion when the city grows).
- **Religious units** (bought with Faith only, only of the buying city's majority religion; see [data/religion.md](data/religion.md#religious-units)):
  - Missionary: 75 Faith (+6 per previous copy), needs a Shrine, M4, 3 spread charges, religious strength 100. Spread Religion adds pressure to the target city equal to 2× its religious strength (RELIGION_SPREAD_STRENGTH_MULTIPLIER = 200%), scaled by the unit's remaining HP (engine; source: epochalmanac link above), and removes 10% of other religions' pressure there (data: Units.ReligionEvictPercent).
  - Apostle: 200 Faith (+15 per previous copy), needs a Temple, M4, 3 spread charges, religious strength 110, removes 25% of other religions on spread. Can Evangelize Belief or Launch Inquisition. Each new Apostle gets one random promotion from: Chaplain (+20 healing to units within 1 tile), Debater (+20 theological strength), Heathen Conversion (a charge converts adjacent barbarians), Indulgence Vendor (+100 Gold when it first converts a city), Martyr (creates a Relic if it dies in theological combat), Orator (+2 spread charges), Pilgrim (+3 charges the first time it moves next to a natural wonder), Proselytizer (spread removes 75% of other religions instead of 25%), Translator (spread is triple strength in other civs' cities).
  - Inquisitor (after Launch Inquisition): 75 Faith (+6 per copy), needs a Temple, 3 charges, religious strength 75. Remove Heresy removes 75% of other religions' pressure in your own cities; it works only inside your territory. Gets +35 theological strength in friendly territory (ability Friendly Territory Advantage).
  - Guru: 120 Faith (+12 per copy), needs a Temple, religious strength 90, 3 heal charges that heal adjacent religious units (COMBAT_HEAL_RELIGIOUS_CHARGE = 40 HP; engine semantics unverified).
  - Warrior Monk (Warrior Monks belief): a military unit, 100 Faith, CS 40, M3, needs a Temple.
  - Great Prophet: founds a religion only; it cannot spread or fight.
- Missionaries, Apostles and Gurus can enter foreign territory without Open Borders (ability Ignores Borders). Inquisitors cannot. A civ can ask you to stop converting its cities (a diplomatic promise).

## Theological combat
- Missionaries, Apostles, Inquisitors and Gurus have religious strength and can attack adjacent religious units of another religion. No war is needed. Damage uses the military damage formula with religious strength in place of combat strength (engine; source: https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Theological_combat_(Civ6)).
- Modifiers: defending next to the defender's own Holy City +15, inside the territory of a city following the defender's religion +5 (data: COMBAT_RELIGIOUS_HOLY_CITY_TILE, COMBAT_RELIGIOUS_FOLLOWING_CITY_TILE; reading confirmed by the fandom link). After Launch Inquisition, all your religious units get +15 in friendly territory (ability Inquisition). Debater +20; Theocracy +5.
- A unit at 0 HP dies. The winner's religion gains 250 pressure and the loser's loses 250 in cities within 6 tiles of the fight (data: RELIGION_SPREAD_COMBAT_VICTORY, RELIGION_SPREAD_RANGE_COMBAT_VICTORY). Monastic Isolation cancels the loss.
- Military units of a civ at war with the religious unit's owner can **Condemn Heretic** to destroy it. That removes 125 pressure of the victim's religion from cities within 6 tiles (data: RELIGION_SPREAD_UNIT_CAPTURE, RELIGION_SPREAD_RANGE_UNIT_CAPTURE).

## Holy cities and relics
- The Holy City gives ×4 passive pressure and +15 theological defence nearby. Behaviour when it is conquered or loses its majority religion: engine; unverified.
- Relics: Great Works that go in Relic slots (Temple, Stave Church/Prasat replacements, Mont St. Michel ×2) or the Palace's general slot. Each gives +4 Faith and +8 Tourism (data: GreatWorks / GreatWork_YieldChanges). Relic tourism needs no tech. Sources: Martyr Apostles, goody huts, some leader/civ abilities.

## Religious victory
A player wins when their religion is the majority religion in every major civilization still in the game. A civ counts as converted when more than half of its cities have that majority religion (engine; source: https://civilization.fandom.com/wiki/Religion_(Civ6)). The player must have founded that religion.

## Integration points
- Religion lens UI shows pressure and majority.
- Diplomacy: AI with "religious" agendas dislike spreading.
- Policy cards and governments affect faith purchase costs.
- Tourism: GlobalParameters has TOURISM_FROM_HOLY_CITY = 8 and TOURISM_DIFFERENT_RELIGION_REDUCTION = 50. These appear to mean Holy City tourism and a 50% cut toward civs with a different majority religion (engine; unverified).
