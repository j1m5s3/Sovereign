# 08 Diplomacy, City-States, Governors, Espionage, World Congress

Reference tables: [data/diplomacy-espionage.md](data/diplomacy-espionage.md) (actions, wars, alliance effects, spy missions, quests), [data/city-states.md](data/city-states.md) (every city-state, suzerain bonus, envoy tier modifiers), [data/governors.md](data/governors.md) (all governors, promotions, title sources), [data/world-congress-emergencies.md](data/world-congress-emergencies.md) (resolutions, emergencies, competitions, rewards), [data/global-parameters.md](data/global-parameters.md) (DIPLOMACY_*, ALLIANCE_*, GRIEVANCES_*, FAVOR_*, ESPIONAGE_*, WORLD_CONGRESS_*, INFLUENCE_*, LEVY_*).

## Meeting and relationship states
- Players meet when units/cities see each other. Before meeting: no interaction.
- Relationship states (data: DiplomaticStates, with AI relationship level): Allied (100), Declared Friend (83), Friendly (66), Neutral (50), Unfriendly (33), Denounced (16), At War (0). Plus an **opinion score** (sum of modifiers with reasons and decay) that drives AI behavior; display as emoji state.
- **Access level** (Diplomatic Visibility: how much you see about another civ, e.g. agendas, gossip, cities) (data: Visibilities): None → Limited → Open → Secret → Top Secret. Meeting gives Limited; each active source adds one level (data: DiplomaticVisibilitySources): the Printing tech, a trade route to them, a delegation or embassy with them, an alliance, a spy in their territory (an extra level if the spy is level 3+), the Great Merchant Mary Katherine Goddard, and some civ traits. Open reveals the historical agenda; Secret lets espionage see their capital; Top Secret all their cities (data: Visibilities_XP2). Gossip messages about their activity are revealed according to access.

## Diplomatic actions (data each with prereq, cost, duration, AI acceptance)
Durations are 30 turns unless noted (data: DiplomaticActions.Duration; DIPLOMACY_*_TIME_LIMIT = 30).

| Action | Prereq | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Send Delegation | met; land path to their capital; obsolete after Diplomatic Service | 25 Gold; +opinion; +1 access level (Delegate source); AI may refuse |
| Resident Embassy [R&F] | Diplomatic Service civic | 50 Gold; +1 access level (Delegate source); with a Diplomatic Quarter [GS] +1 Favor per turn per embassy (and per delegation) |
| Open Borders | Early Empire civic | units can enter their territory; 30 turns; +25% tourism toward the civ granting it |
| Declaration of Friendship | met; AI must be Friendly (no civic needed) | 30 turns; cannot declare war on each other without penalty; no denounce |
| Denounce | met | lasts 30 turns; a formal war needs 5 turns of denouncement first (data: DIPLOMACY_DENOUNCE_WAR_DELAY); opinion penalty; [GS] generates 25 grievances (data: GRIEVANCES_FOR_DENOUNCEMENT) |
| Alliance [R&F] | Civil Service civic for both; must be Declared Friends (engine) | Types: Research, Military, Economic, Cultural, Religious. 30 turns, renewable. Alliance points accumulate → levels 1–3 (see below); shared visibility at Military level 2; shared wars (call to arms) |
| Joint War | Foreign Trade civic | coordinated war declaration with a third party (also "join ongoing war") |
| Trade Deal | met | see 07 |
| Make Demand | met | AI may yield items if weaker |
| Declare War | see war types | — |
| Make Peace | at war ≥ 10 turns (data: DIPLOMACY_WAR_MIN_TURNS) | peace treaty with items (cities, gold, resources); peace then lasts at least 10 turns (Make Peace duration 10) |
| Ask Promise | met | request: stop settling near, stop converting, stop spying, stop digging artifacts. [GS] asking costs 30 Favor; refusing gives 25 grievances; breaking a promise multiplies the grievances of the act by 200% (data: DiplomaticActions_XP2, GRIEVANCE_MULTIPLIER_FOR_BROKEN_PROMISE) |

**Alliance levels** [R&F]: points accrue at 1 per turn, +0.25 for having at least one route to the ally and +0.25 for receiving one (stored internally ×4: data ALLIANCE_POINTS_MULTIPLIER = 4, ALLIANCE_POINTS_FOR_TRADE = 1). Level 2 at 80 points, level 3 at 240 (data: ALLIANCE_LEVEL_TWO_XP = 320, ALLIANCE_LEVEL_THREE_XP = 960 in internal units; source for per-turn reading: https://www.civilopedia.net/en-US/gathering-storm/concepts/alliances_1/). Effects (data: AllianceEffects):

| Type | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | routes with ally: +2 Science origin, +1 destination | research agreement (30) | share 10% of ally's Science |
| Military | +5 Combat Strength vs common war targets | shared visibility; +15% military unit Production while either is at war | units trained gain a free promotion's XP |
| Economic | routes with ally: +4 Gold origin, +2 destination | +1 envoy point from ally's city-state tributaries | share ally's suzerain bonuses |
| Cultural | routes: +2 Culture origin, +1 destination; no loyalty pressure between allies | +1 GPP from districts in cities with a route to ally | share 20% tourism and 10% Culture |
| Religious | routes: +2 Faith origin, +1 destination; no religious pressure between allies | +10 religious Combat Strength vs other religions | +1 Faith per ally-religion follower; +20 pressure |

## War
- **War types** (data: Wars, DiplomaticActions). Warmonger/grievance percent of a normal declaration in brackets [GS uses it to scale grievances]:
  - Surprise War: declared without 5 turns of denouncement [150%].
  - Formal War: after ≥5 turns of denouncement [100%].
  - Joint War / joining an ongoing war (Foreign Trade) [100%].
  - **Casus Belli** (need the civic and condition; all require 5 turns of denouncement except Protectorate and Golden Age):
    - Holy War (Diplomatic Service; they converted one of your cities) [50%].
    - Liberation War (Diplomatic Service; they hold a city of a friend/ally) [0% for the declaration].
    - Reconquest War (Defensive Tactics; they hold a city you used to own) [0%].
    - Protectorate War (Defensive Tactics; they are at war with a city-state you are suzerain of) [0%].
    - War of Retribution [GS] (Early Empire; they broke a promise to you) [50%].
    - Colonial War (Nationalism; target is 2 technology eras behind you) [50%].
    - War of Territorial Expansion (Mobilization; 2 of your cities within 10 tiles of 2 of theirs; data: DIPLOMACY_ADJACENT_EMPIRE_*) [75%].
    - Ideological War [GS] (Ideology civic; both in different tier-3+ governments) [50%].
    - Golden Age War [R&F] (in a Golden Age with the "To Arms!" dedication; usable right after denouncing) [25%].
  - Other war contexts in data: Defensive Pact, Suzerain War (city-state joins its suzerain), Emergency War.
- **Grievances** [GS replaces warmonger]: grievances against a civ accrue from war declarations (scaled by the war type), capturing/razing cities (data: CaptureWarmongerPercent/RazeWarmongerPercent), holding someone's cities (+3 per turn per capital, +1 per other city; data: GRIEVANCES_POSSESS_*), denouncing (25), declaring war on a city-state you hold envoys in (50) or are suzerain of (100), conquering a city-state (50 with every civ), nukes, broken promises (×200%) and espionage caught. Allies share 50% and declared friends 25% of war grievances (data: SHARE_WAR_GRIEVANCES_*). Grievances decay each turn at a rate that falls with the era (data: Eras_XP2.GrievanceDecayRate 10 Ancient → 2 Future). Grievances held against a civ affect AI opinion, reduce war weariness for those fighting it, open casus belli and drain its Diplomatic Favor (see below).
- **War weariness**: points from combat (+2 per combat in foreign lands, +1 in allied lands), units lost (+3 each), WMDs launched (+10); decays 50 per turn at war, 200 per turn at peace, and drops 2000 when peace is made (data: WAR_WEARINESS_*). Effect: −1 Amenity per 400 points in affected cities (data: WAR_WEARINESS_POINTS_FOR_AMENITY_LOSS). Reduced by grievances against the enemy and policies (e.g., Propaganda); Fascism +20%.
- Peace can only be proposed after 10 turns of war and then holds at least 10 turns (data: DIPLOMACY_WAR_MIN_TURNS, DIPLOMACY_PEACE_MIN_TURNS). Peace deals include ceding cities. City-states at war persist.
- Occupied cities: −amenities until peace grants them.

## Agendas (AI personality)
- Each AI leader has 1 **historical agenda** and 1 **random agenda** (data: AGENDAS_MAJOR_CIV_HISTORICAL_AGENDAS = 1, _RANDOM_AGENDAS = 1). The historical one is shown once access is high enough; examples: Trajan "Optimus Princeps" likes large territories; Montezuma "Tlatoani" likes civs with the same luxuries as him and dislikes those with luxuries he lacks; Gandhi "Peacekeeper" likes peaceful civs and hates warmongers; Cleopatra "Queen of the Nile" likes strong militaries.
- Random (hidden) agendas come from a pool of 34 (data: RandomAgendas), e.g. Air Power, City-State Ally, Civilized, Cultured, Darwinist, Devout, Environmentalist, Exploitative, Explorer, Great White Fleet, Money Grubber, Nuke Happy, Paranoid, Pillager, Standing Army, Sycophant, Technophile, Wonder Obsessed. [GS] Each is tagged early-era, late-era or any-era and is re-rolled to fit the era (data: RandomAgendas_XP2, RandomAgendasInEra). Revealed through access/gossip.
- Agendas produce opinion modifiers each turn (+/−), shaping AI deal acceptance and war decisions.

## City-States
- Minor civilizations (one city, cannot expand). Six types (8 of each in this ruleset): Scientific, Cultural, Religious, Trade, Industrial, Militaristic. Each has a unique suzerain bonus (list in [data/city-states.md](data/city-states.md)).
- **Envoys** come from: being the first major civ to meet the city-state (+1, data: INFLUENCE_TOKENS_FREE_FOR_FIRST_PLAYER_MEET), civics (Mysticism, Military Training, Theology, Naval Tradition, Mercenaries +1 each; Colonialism, Opera and Ballet, Natural History, Scorched Earth +2; Conservation, Cultural Heritage, Near Future Governance, Global Warming Mitigation +3), Influence Points (each government has points per turn, a threshold and envoys per threshold: Chiefdom 1/100/1, tier 1 3/100/1, tier 2 5/150/2, tier 3 7/200/3, tier 4 9/250/4; data: Governments; Monarchy +50% envoys; policies such as Charismatic Leader add points), quests, Great People (Great Merchants Piero de' Bardi, Jakob Fugger, John Jacob Astor, Zhou Daguan, Raja Todar Mal; Great General Amina), Diplomatic Quarter, Amani. Sending an envoy to a met city-state is permanent (except Fabricate Scandal).
- **Envoy tier bonuses** (to each player; only while at peace with that city-state; data: MINOR_CIV_* modifiers):

  | Type | 1 envoy | 3 envoys | 6 envoys |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Scientific | +1 Science in capital and per Library | +2 Science per University | +3 Science per Research Lab |
  | Cultural | +1 Culture in capital and per Amphitheater | +2 Culture per Art Museum and Archaeological Museum | +3 Culture per Broadcast Center |
  | Religious | +1 Faith in capital and per Shrine | +2 Faith per Temple | +3 Faith per worship building (incl. Cathedral) |
  | Trade | +2 Gold in capital and per Market and Lighthouse | +4 Gold per Bank and Shipyard | +6 Gold per Stock Exchange and Seaport |
  | Industrial | +1 Production toward districts and buildings in capital and in cities with a Workshop | +2 in cities with a Factory | +3 in cities with a power plant |
  | Militaristic | +1 Production toward units in capital and in cities with Barracks/Stable | +2 in cities with an Armory | +3 in cities with a Military Academy |

  [GS] Each tier's bonus also applies in cities with the matching Diplomatic Quarter building: Consulate gives the 3-envoy amount, Chancery the 6-envoy amount.
- **Suzerain**: the player with the most envoys, at least 3 (data: INFLUENCE_TOKENS_MINIMUM_FOR_SUZERAIN) and more than anyone else (tie handling engine; unverified). The suzerain gets the unique bonus (e.g., Geneva: +15% Science while at peace with all majors; Kabul: +100% combat XP; Hattusa: +2 per turn of each revealed strategic resource you have not improved; Antananarivo: +2% Culture per Great Person earned; Jerusalem: your cities with a Holy Site exert pressure as if they were Holy Cities (4x); Bandar Brunei: +1 Gold per foreign Trading Post), the city-state's luxury and strategic resources, +1 Diplomatic Favor per turn [GS] (data: WORLD_CONGRESS_SUZERAIN_FAVOR_PER_TURN), and can **Levy Military**: pay 25% of the units' purchase cost to control its units for 30 turns (data: LEVY_MILITARY_*). The city-state joins the suzerain's wars (Suzerain War).
  - Religious and scientific bonuses whose data text is garbled, as the Civilopedia [GS] words them (https://www.civilopedia.net/gathering-storm/citystates/civilization_chinguetti and its neighbours): Chinguetti: domestic and international routes +1 Faith per follower of your founded (or majority) religion "in this city", read as the origin city, as fan guides do; Fez: the first time your religious unit converts a city, +20 Science per citizen of that city (Standard speed); Vatican City: each Great Person activated spreads 400 pressure of your founded (or majority) religion to the cities within 10 tiles.
  - Other bonuses kept as text in the data, as the Civilopedia [GS] and the Civilization wiki word them: Bandar Brunei: your Trading Posts in foreign cities give +1 more Gold to every route passing through them; Buenos Aires: your bonus resources behave like luxuries, 1 Amenity per kind (Sovereign reading: to as many cities as most luxuries, 4); Mexico City: regional effects of your Industrial Zone, Entertainment Complex and Water Park buildings reach 3 tiles farther (power plants included); Mogadishu: your Traders cannot be plundered on water; Wolin: Great General points when a land unit, Great Admiral points when a naval unit, defeats a civ's or city-state's unit, a quarter of its Combat Strength; Yerevan: your Apostles choose their promotion instead of drawing one (Sovereign reading: a normal promotion choice, which ends the Apostle's turn); Zanzibar: the Cinnamon and Cloves luxuries, found nowhere else, 6 cities each. Lahore's Nihang has no unit in the data, so it is left out.
- **Quests** (data: Quests): convert its capital to your religion, send a trade route, clear a barbarian outpost within 5 tiles, train a unit type, build a district type, trigger a given Eureka, trigger a given Inspiration, recruit a Great Person of a class. Reward: envoy(s).
- City-state conquest: Protectorate War CB for suzerain; capturing it removes it and generates grievances with everyone and a City-State Emergency [GS].
- City-state AI: defends itself, has units, spawns based on era.
- **Unique improvements** unlocked for the suzerain (keep as data): Colossal Head (La Venta), Moai (Rapa Nui), Alcázar (Granada), Nazca Line (Nazca), Mahavihara (Nalanda), Batey (Caguana), Cahokia Mounds (Cahokia), Trading Dome (Samarkand), Monastery (Armagh).

## Governors [R&F]
- **Governor titles** (data: governor-title sources): one each from the civics State Workforce, Early Empire, Defensive Tactics, Recorded History, Guilds, Medieval Faires, Civil Engineering, Nationalism, Mass Media, Mobilization, Globalization, Social Media, Near Future Governance and Future Civic — 14 in total — plus the Casa de Contratación wonder (Spain, +3), Great People (Irene of Athens, Adam Smith, Francisco de Paula Santander) and Suleiman's trait (+1). A title either appoints a new governor or promotes an existing one.
- Governors (base ability + 5 promotions; tree layout in [data/governors.md](data/governors.md)):
  - **Victor (Castellan)**: Redoubt (base: +5 city defense; faster to establish); Garrison Commander, Defense Logistics, Embrasure (extra city attack per turn; units trained get a free promotion's XP), Air Defense Initiative, Arms Race Proponent (+30% Production toward nuclear projects).
  - **Amani (Diplomat)**: Messenger (base: can serve in a city-state, counting as 2 envoys); Emissary (+2 loyalty pressure), Affluence (copies the city-state's luxuries when suzerain), Local Informants (+3 spy levels against this city), Foreign Investor (copies its strategics), Puppeteer (doubles Amani's envoys).
  - **Magnus (Steward)**: Groundbreaker (base: +50% harvest/chop yields); Surplus Logistics (+20% growth; foreign routes to the city give +2 Food), Provision (Settlers do not consume population), Industrialist (+2 Production per power plant), Black Marketeer (units need 80% less strategic upkeep), Vertical Integration (city receives regional Production from all same-type buildings).
  - **Liang (Surveyor)**: Guildmaster (base: Builders trained here +1 charge); Zoning Commissioner (+20% district Production), Aquaculture (Fishery improvement), Reinforced Materials (immune to disaster damage), Water Works (Neighborhood/Aqueduct +2 Housing; Canal/Dam +1 Amenity), Parks and Recreation (City Park improvement).
  - **Pingala (Educator)**: Librarian (base: +15% Science and Culture); Connoisseur (+1 Culture per citizen), Researcher (+1 Science per citizen), Grants (+100% GPP in city), Curator (×2 tourism from Great Works here), Space Initiative (+30% space project Production).
  - **Reyna (Financier)**: Land Acquisition (base: faster border growth, +3 Gold per foreign route passing through); Harbormaster (doubles Commercial Hub and Harbor adjacency), Forestry Management (+2 Gold on unimproved feature tiles), Tax Collector (+2 Gold per citizen), Contractor (buy districts with Gold), Renewable Subsidizer [GS] (+2 Gold per renewable-energy improvement and Hydroelectric Dam).
  - **Moksha (Cardinal)**: Bishop (base: religious pressure doubled from this city, +2 Faith per district); Grand Inquisitor (+10 religious combat for units bought here), Laying On Of Hands (full religious unit heal), Citadel of God (immune to foreign pressure and religious combat; faith refund of 25% of building cost), Patron Saint (Apostles get an extra promotion), Divine Architect (buy districts with Faith).
  - **Ibrahim (Grand Vizier)** [GS, Ottoman pack]: unique to Suleiman (Ottomans), not available to other players. Pasha (base: +20% military unit Production); Head Falconer, Serasker, Khass-Oda-Bashi (alliance points), Capou Agha, Grand Vizier. Can be assigned to another major civ's capital or a city-state.
- Assigning to a city: governor takes 5 turns to establish (data: GOVERNOR_BASE_TURNS_TO_ESTABLISH), shortened for governors with higher TransitionStrength (Victor and Ibrahim 150 vs 100 → about 3 turns; engine; unverified exact formula). Abilities are inactive until established; moving resets. Established governor: +8 loyalty per turn in its city (data: Governors.IdentityPressure).
- Spies can neutralize governors (base 6 turns inactive, data: ESPIONAGE_NEUTRALIZE_GOVERNOR_BASE_TURNS).

## Espionage
- Spies (225 Production, +75 per previous spy; 4 Gold maintenance). Spy capacity +1 each from the civics Diplomatic Service (first spy), Nationalism, Ideology and Cold War, the Computers tech, and the Intelligence Agency government building (which also gives +1 level to new spies).
- Spies travel to a city (3 turns), then perform missions (data: UnitOperations; turns at Standard speed):
  - Defensive/passive: Counterspy (16 turns; defends a district in your city), Listening Post (8; reveals gossip, +access), Gain Sources (8; intel, and +2 levels to future missions in that city for 24 turns: data ESPIONAGE_BONUS_GAIN_SOURCES = 2, duration ×3).
  - Offensive (base difficulty in brackets): Siphon Funds (Commercial Hub) [13], Steal Tech Boost (Campus) [14], Sabotage Production (Industrial Zone) [14], Neutralize Governor [14], Great Work Heist (Theater Square) [15], Disrupt Rocketry (Spaceport) [15], Breach Dam (Dam) [GS] [15], Recruit Partisans (Neighborhood) [16], Foment Unrest (city; −15 loyalty −5 per spy level) [13], Fabricate Scandal (16 turns; removes 2 envoys +1 per level of the target's at a city-state) [13].
- Success: the UI shows outcomes from a fixed ladder of success chances (16, 25, 37, 50, 63, 74, 84, 90%); a recruit with no help gets 50% on Siphon Funds, 37% on Steal Tech/Sabotage, 25% on Heist/Rocketry, 16% on Partisans; each spy level or bonus level moves one step up (engine; source: https://forums.civfanatics.com/threads/effect-of-spy-level.624830/). Those values match a 3d6 roll needing ≥ (base − 2 − bonus levels), so treat BaseProbability as that target number (inference; engine; unverified). Data also lists +3 difficulty when a counterspy defends and +1 per counterspy level (data: EnemyProbChange, EnemyLevelProbChange), and Chancery/police effects. Outcomes: success unseen; success but detected; failure but escaped; failure and captured or killed. Escape chance base 10 (data: ESPIONAGE_ESCAPE_*; Police State −4, Ace Driver +1). Captured spies can be traded back (Captive deal item).
- Levels (data: ESPIONAGE_MAX_LEVEL = 4): Spy Recruit, Agent, Secret Agent, Master Spy. Promotions: Ace Driver, Cat Burglar, Con Artist, Covert Action, Demolitions, Disguise, Guerrilla Leader, License to Kill, Linguist, Polygraph, Quartermaster, Rocket Scientist, Satchel Charges, Seduction, Smear Campaign, Surveillance, Technologist.

## Diplomatic Favor and World Congress [GS]
- **Diplomatic Favor** per turn: government (data: Governments_XP2.Favor: Chiefdom 0, tier 1 +1, tier 2 +2, tier 3 +3, tier 4 +4), +1 per alliance, +1 per city-state you are suzerain of (Országház doubles it), policies (Diplomatic Capital +4, Rabblerousing +1), Monarchy +2 per city with Renaissance Walls, Diplomatic Quarter (+1 per delegation and per embassy you hold), Foreign Ministry government building +3, Pagoda worship building +1, Future Civic one-off +50, and one-off grants: liberating a city-state or player city +100, reviving a player +200, emergency/competition rewards (+50 to +200). Lost per turn: grievances held against you above 200 cost about 1 per 50 grievances, up to −10 (data: FAVOR_GRIEVANCES_START/_DIVISOR/_MINIMUM; engine reading unverified), and CO2 emissions up to −20 (data: FAVOR_CO2_*; engine reading unverified). Also gained/spent in trades.
- **World Congress**: first convenes once the first civ reaches the Medieval era (data: WORLD_CONGRESS_INITIAL_ERA = 2, zero-based), then at most every 30 turns (data: WORLD_CONGRESS_MAX_TIME_BETWEEN_MEETINGS; scaled by speed). Each session votes on **2 resolutions** drawn from the era-eligible pool (engine), each with option A/B and a target (player, district, resource, yield, great-person class, policy slot type, etc.). Pool (data: Resolutions, full effects in [data/world-congress-emergencies.md](data/world-congress-emergencies.md)): Luxury Policy, Trade Policy, World Religion, Mercenary Companies, Arms Control, Heritage Organization, World Ideology, Urban Development Treaty, Border Control Treaty, Public Works Program, Patronage, Global Energy Treaty, Sovereignty, Migration Treaty, Deforestation Treaty, Public Relations, Espionage Pact, Military Advisory, plus the injected Diplomatic Victory resolution (Modern era onward). Each player gets 1 free vote per resolution and can buy more with favor at escalating cost (10, 20, 30… per extra vote, i.e. 10, 30, 60, 100, 150 cumulative) (engine; source: https://twinfinite.net/guides/civ-6-gathering-storm-world-congress-works/).
- **Special sessions** (at least 15 turns apart; data: WORLD_CONGRESS_MIN_TIME_BETWEEN_SPECIAL_SESSIONS) for emergencies, Aid Requests (after a disaster) and Military Aid Requests; calling an emergency costs favor (data: FAVOR_COST_FOR_EMERGENCY 30, FAVOR_COST_FOR_REQUEST 20).
- **Scored Competitions**: World's Fair, World Games, Climate Accords, International Space Station, Nobel Prize in Literature / Physics / Peace Prize, Aid Request, Military Aid Request; first place and tier rewards in data.
- **Diplomatic Victory**: first to **20 Diplomatic Victory Points** (data: DIPLOMATIC_VICTORY_POINTS_REQUIRED). Sources (data): the Diplomatic Victory resolution (+2 to the target if A passes, −2 if B), first place in Aid Request, Military Aid Request or Climate Accords (+2), first place in World's Fair, World Games, International Space Station or Nobel Peace Prize (+1), Mahabodhi Temple +2, Potala Palace +1, Statue of Liberty +4, the Seasteads tech +1, the Global Warming Mitigation civic +1. Points can also be lost via the resolution.

## Emergencies [R&F]
Triggered (data: Emergencies) when a civ: captures a city of another civ (Military Emergency), captures a city-state (City-State Emergency), converts a Holy City (Religious Emergency), uses a nuclear weapon (Nuclear Emergency), or declares war on an ally/friend (Betrayal Emergency) [GS]. Eligible civs choose to join; the target civ receives the opposite goal. Duration 30 turns (Nuclear and Betrayal 60). Success gives members rewards (e.g., +100 Favor, relics, combat bonuses); failure gives the target rewards (e.g., +200 Favor). Competitions in GS run 29 turns.
