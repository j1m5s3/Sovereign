# Plan: the remaining Civ systems (step 6, item 4)

Status: active, 2026-10-05. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-05-battle-ai.md`.

Goal: James's item 4, the Civ VI systems the core does not have yet, built to `specs/civ6/` (Sovereign docs override). The order follows dependencies, not the list order: ships and the water districts come first because movement, the AI, Harbor great admirals, trade by sea and coastal wonders all need them; great people come before religion because a Great Prophet founds a religion.

## Milestones (one PR each)

1. **Naval and embarkation:** sea movement and pathing (Coast; Ocean after Cartography), naval units trained in coastal cities, naval melee and ranged combat, embarking (Shipbuilding; Builders after Sailing), embarked strength by era, the embark cost, naval healing, and the Harbor district with its buildings. AI: trains a few ships and keeps them home; embarks to settle and attack across water later. *Built 2026-10-05:* all of it except the AI's ships. The AI does not train ships yet, and its land units march overland (a new `overland` move option), because embarked armies cannot attack and defend at 10 to 15. Teaching the AI to use the sea is a follow-up. Harbor buildings: coast-tile yields as modifiers; their +25% XP for trained ships waits for per-city XP modifiers.
2. **Great people:** points from districts and buildings, the per-era pool with rising costs, patronage with gold or faith, recruitment, and the effects that touch systems already built (generals and admirals as auras, engineers and merchants with direct effects). The rest get their effects when their system arrives. *Built 2026-10-05:* all 202 individuals load from `data/rules/greatpeople.json`, which the generator builds from great-people.md. Their activation effects become typed effects where the core can apply them: yields, production, Eurekas and Inspirations, promotion XP, buildings, units, building yields and points. Effects of systems still to come (trade, envoys, governors, loyalty, wonders) are kept as text. Great Works fill building slots, with each type's typical yield. Tourism waits for the culture victory. Recruitment uses the shared timeline (older individuals drop out as the world era advances) and a +30% cost per era ahead. Prophets (one each) wait for religion to be usable. The AI walks its great people to where they work, buys with spare gold, and now values districts and their great person points, so it builds far more of them.
3. **Religion:** faith, pantheons, the Great Prophet and founding, beliefs, missionaries and apostles, pressure and conversion, theological combat, holy cities, religious victory. *Built 2026-10-05:* beliefs, religions and religious unit stats are generated into `data/rules/religion.json`. Belief effects that fit the core are hand-written modifiers with `BELIEF_*` sources, using new effect kinds for founder and enhancer beliefs. The rest show as "not in the game yet". Pantheons cost 25 Faith; Fertility Rites and Religious Settlements grant their unit. Prophets found religions on Holy Sites, up to 3 on a Tiny map, and stop appearing once all are founded. Apostles evangelize one belief per class. Missionaries and Apostles are bought with Faith and spread religion: twice their strength, scaled by health, evicting others. Passive pressure works as in Civ (10 tiles, x2 Holy Site, x4 Holy City), with followers counted against unbelief. Theological combat needs no war; the winner gains 250 pressure and the loser loses 250 within 6 tiles. Worship buildings are bought with Faith only (they could be built with production before; fixed). Religious victory is in. Follow-ups: the Inquisition, Gurus, Warrior Monks, Condemn Heretic pressure, relics and the remaining belief effects. The AI races for one Holy Site while religions remain, takes a pantheon, founds, evangelizes, buys and sends missionaries.
4. **Trade routes:** Traders, route yields by destination, capacity from Markets and Lighthouses, roads built by traders, trade by sea. *Built 2026-10-05:* capacity comes from Foreign Trade, Markets, and Lighthouses in cities without a Market. Route yields by destination district come from the generated data (domestic and international). Range is 15 tiles by land, or 30 once Traders can sail. A route lasts 20 turns plus the world era's extra, scaled by speed; its Trader is off the map meanwhile and comes home at the end. Routes lay roads; the road tier follows the era, a road replaces terrain cost, and later tiers bridge rivers. War with the destination ends a route. A raider at war on the road plunders it for 100 gold (Civ amount unverified). Routes carry religious pressure to the destination. A Trader may start from its city or a tile beside it on the city's land (Sovereign convenience). Follow-ups: Trading Posts and range refuelling, the origin-side religious pressure, policy route bonuses, city-state routes.
5. **Wonders:** placement and production, effects through modifiers, the ones that touch systems already built.
6. **City-states and envoys:** city-state players, envoys and suzerainty bonuses, quests left for later.
7. **Eras and ages:** era score and historic moments, golden, normal and dark ages with dedications; tourism and the culture victory.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Rules stay data: new districts, units and buildings come from `tools/rules_gen` (extend `PLACEABLE_DISTRICTS` and friends), never hand-edited generated JSON; hand-written tuning goes in the hand-written files.
- Each milestone bumps the save version and regenerates the golden hash.
- AI behaviour for each system is simple and rule-following first; deeper planning arrives with the strategy AI work (leader doc §10, layer 1).
