# Plan: city projects, the Science victory, air power and canals (step 6, "everything else")

Status: active, 2026-10-05. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-05-player-modelling.md`. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent: the Science victory is the one Civ victory the core lacks, and it rests on city projects, the Spaceport and (for the late game around it) air units and the remaining districts. James can redirect at any point.

Specs: 03-districts-buildings-wonders (Projects; district rows for Spaceport, Aerodrome, Canal) with data/projects and data/districts; 09-civs-eras-victory-climate (Victory conditions: 1. Science); 05-units-and-combat (air units, air combat, anti-air) with data/units.

## Milestones (one PR each)

1. **Done: City projects:** a fourth production kind generated from data/projects: district projects that turn part of the city's production into a yield while they run and grant great person points when done (Campus Research Grants, Holy Site Prayers, Theater Square Festival, Commercial Hub Investment, Harbor Shipping, Industrial Zone Logistics, Encampment Training), Bread and Circuses (loyalty), Carbon Recapture (favor, CO2 removed), Repair Outer Defenses; cost growth with game progress, once-per-player limits and prerequisites; never bought with gold. AI and Unreal production lists.
2. **Done: The Spaceport and the Science victory:** the Spaceport district (flat land); Launch Earth Satellite (reveals the map), Moon Landing (culture worth 10 turns of science), Mars Colony, Exoplanet Expedition (1 light-year a turn), Lagrange and Terrestrial Laser Stations (+1 each); the expedition wins at 50 light-years. AI: the Science strategy builds the Spaceport and the chain; HUD progress for every civ.
3. **Done (this PR): Air power:** the Aerodrome (air slots), air units based in cities and aerodromes (rebase, air strike, deploy as fighters on patrol), interception and anti-air, bombers against districts and cities; AI use.
4. **The Canal:** a district linking two bodies of water (or water and the City Center) that lets ships and naval trade pass; the Industrial Zone adjacency it gives already exists in the data.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Milestone 1 as built: `projects.json` from data/projects (generic districts only; Yongle's Lijia and unique-district projects left out); `ProductionKind::Project`; `ProjectType` with district, unlock, cost and GAME_PROGRESS growth (x (1 + param/100 x the larger tree share), so district projects run 25 -> 400), once-per-player limits, a prerequisite project, a yield conversion, great person points and a resource cost; typed completion effects (repair walls, loyalty, favor, CO2 removed, reveal the map, culture from science, expedition speed). A project whose effects the core cannot carry (nuclear devices and the Manhattan Project, power conversions and decommissioning, Send Aid, Train Athletes and Astronauts, Court Festival, Recommission Reactor) is marked not modelled and never offered. Conversion: while a converting project is first in the queue, the city report adds its share of production as that yield (so it flows into science, culture, faith or gold like any yield); great person points arrive on completion. `Player::projectsDone` (save version 33). AI: district projects fill a city with nothing better (value 30 + 2 per GPP + the conversion share), Bread and Circuses below 60 loyalty, Repair Outer Defenses when threatened, Carbon Recapture when the civ emits over a quarter of the world's CO2. Unreal lists projects in the production chooser.
- Milestone 2 as built: the Spaceport district (cost 1800, no progression; flat Grassland, Plains, Tundra, Snow or Desert via a generated `validTerrains`); the Spaceport's "rocket" buildings are internal art markers and are left out of `buildings.json`. `Victory::Science` and `GameSetup::scienceVictory` (save version 34); `Player::lightYears` grows each world turn by `Game::expeditionSpeed` (the expedition's 1 plus 1 per laser station completed; the Terrestrial station counts as powered, power is not modelled); the expedition wins at SCIENCE_VICTORY_POINTS_REQUIRED (50); the International Space Station competition is not modelled. AI: one Spaceport per civ in a productive city (value 4000 under the Science strategy, 1500 otherwise, + production), space projects 1500/800; the Science strategy turns exclusive once its expedition flies and is disqualified when the victory is off. HUD: every civ's expedition progress; sovsim names the victory. Deity all-AI games: a Science victory on turn 348 (seed 2).
- Milestone 3 as built (`src/air.cpp`): generated `antiAir` on units, `airSlots` on districts (City Center 1, Aerodrome 2) and buildings (Hangar, Airport +1); the Aerodrome district (flat land). Aircraft get their own `UnitLayer::Air`: they never stack with, garrison or defend a plot, cannot walk (MoveUnit is refused) and are built only into a free slot (the center first, then the Aerodrome). `Command::rebaseUnit` moves one to a friendly base with room within twice its strike range (Sovereign reading) and spends its turn. Air strikes use the ranged attack from the base within the unit's range, need the target visible but no line of sight; before the strike the strongest cover hits the aircraft (`Game::interception`: an enemy fighter fortified as on patrol within its range, using its combat strength, or an anti-air unit within one plot, using its anti-air strength, against the aircraft's combat strength); a downed aircraft never strikes. Aircraft at a captured or flipped city's center or Aerodrome are lost. AI: a small air force at war or under Domination (fighters first), strikes skip skies held by stronger cover, idle aircraft patrol and rebase toward the target city, Aerodromes at war. Unreal: right-click rebases a selected aircraft. Not yet: carriers, the Airstrip, air pillage, air-to-air sweeps and the bomber's penalty against units (bombers simply hit hard), aircraft art.
- Projects reuse the production queue and progress (a project is just another item); a finished project leaves the queue like any item, and a repeatable one is simply queued again.
