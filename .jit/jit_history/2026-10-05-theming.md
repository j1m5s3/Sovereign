# Plan: theming bonuses (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-05. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-05-military-aid.md`. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent: museum theming from 07, left out of `2026-10-05-archaeology.md` because artifacts had no era or civilization. Three milestones.

Specs: 07-great-people-great-works-tourism (Theming bonuses); data/buildings (Great Work slots and theming).

## Milestone

1. **Done** (generated `theming` on buildings; `BuildingType::theming`; `GreatWork::era`, `GreatWork::civ`, `GameState::battleHistory`, save version 56; `Game::themed`):
   - **Generated rules.** `tools/rules_gen` reads the theming flags and percents from the buildings table. Only the Art Museum and the Archaeological Museum have them.
   - **When a building is themed.**
     - Every one of its slots holds a work.
     - Art Museum: all works are of one object type (Sculpture, Portrait, Landscape, Religious), each by a different artist.
     - Archaeological Museum: all artifacts are from one era, each from a different civilization.
   - **The bonus.** A themed building's works give +100% yield and +100% tourism.
   - **Where an artifact's history comes from.**
     - Every early battle site records the world era and the attacker's civilization, and the artifact dug there carries both.
     - A site placed on open ground gives a random major civ's civilization and a random era up to ARCHAEOLOGY_MAX_ERA, drawn when dug.

2. **Done** (command `MoveGreatWork` 61; `Game::freeSlotsFor`, `moveGreatWorkProblem`, `moveGreatWork`, `themingMoves`):
   - **Moving works.** A civ moves any of its Great Works to a free, compatible slot in any of its cities.
   - **`themingMoves`.** It plans the moves that theme a museum: it picks a theme (object type or era) that the civ's works can fill with distinct artists or civilizations. Works not chosen are moved out first, to free slots elsewhere. Works in other themed buildings are left alone.
   - **AI.** It applies those moves every turn.
   - **Unreal.** The production chooser offers "Theme the <museum> (N moves)", which sends them all.

3. **Done** (`DealItemKind::GreatWork`: amount = the city holding the work, resource = its index there; `Game::dealWork`, `workCompletesTheme`):
   - **The deal item.** Great Works trade in deals when the taker has a free slot for them.
   - **How the AI values one.** It values a work it receives at 60 + 20 × tourism, plus 300 if the work completes one of its museum themes. Giving a work costs it 1.5× that value, and a work in a themed museum is never for sale.
   - **AI offers.** The AI offers gold for another civ's work that would complete one of its themes.
   - **Unreal.** Deals describe the work ("a Great Work (sculpture by …)"), and AI offers to the player show up as usual.
   - **Diplomacy module.** The persona lists "a Great Work", but the talk parser does not take Great Works yet: they have no stable names.
   - **Result.** About 7 themed museums in a 6-AI soak, up from 0.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Themed museums stay rare in AI games: each artist makes three works of one type, and a civ seldom holds artifacts of one era from three civilizations. Trading (milestone 3) is what makes it happen.
- An artifact belongs to the attacker's civilization (Civ VI records both sides; one is enough for "different civilizations").
