# Record: the map and terrain spec audited again against the core

Status: done, 2026-10-10. Previous: `2026-10-10-districts-audit-2.md`. A second read of specs/civ6/01-map-and-terrain.md (the first is `2026-10-09-map-audit.md`) against the core. Terrain and feature yields, movement, river crossing, embarking, Appeal, sight, harvests, improvement placement, reveal techs, strategic stockpiles, luxuries and fresh water match. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent.

## Fixes

- **Mount Everest marks a land unit that ends its turn beside it** (01), not one that only passes by; the check moved from `Game::enterPlot` to the start of the owner's next turn in `Game::beginPlayerTurn`.
- **James Young's early Oil yields** its Production: `plotYields` asked for the reveal tech while `Game::resourceVisible` already counted James Young's Oil.
- **Start positions weigh nearby luxuries** (01: "score tiles for ... nearby luxuries"): +10 for each kind of luxury within 3 plots in `startScore`, a Sovereign weight a little under fresh water's 15.

## Left open

- Per-region resource fairness (each start its own luxury types, Horses and Iron near balanced starts) is not built: map generation places resources by frequency over the whole map. A larger piece of map generation, close to the start-balance setup options left out in `2026-10-06-spec-audit-gaps.md`.
- Districts on Geothermal Fissures and Oases: the data's "district ok" marks only Floodplains and Volcanic Soil, but covers expansion features only; not changed.
- Harvest yields and production-for-buildings bonuses (an unverified engine rule in the spec).
- Natural wonder counts per map size are written in `placeNaturalWonders`; they match the data.

## Results

- 128 AI games (Small, 6 AI, turn 200) against the districts audit: every figure within one and a half standard errors (start plots move, so every game differs).
- 8 long AI games in 8 setups (up to Huge, 400 turns) end with no crash or replay mismatch; the saved and reloaded runs end on the straight game's state.
- Mutation: every mutant caught.
- The 30-turn golden game's state hash file regenerated (start plots changed).
