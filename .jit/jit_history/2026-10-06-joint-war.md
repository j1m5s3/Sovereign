# Plan: Joint War (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-06. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-06-captured-spies.md`. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent: "Not modelled: Joint War invitations" in `2026-10-05-casus-belli.md`. One milestone, one PR.

Specs: 08-diplomacy-city-states-governors (Diplomatic actions: "Joint War | Foreign Trade civic | coordinated war declaration with a third party (also 'join ongoing war')"; War types: Joint War [100%]); data/diplomacy-espionage (Joint War: Foreign Trade, no denouncement required, warmonger 100); 07 (deal items: agreements include joint war).

## Milestone

1. **Done: Joint War as a deal item** (`DealItemKind::JointWar`, amount = the target): two civs at peace with each other agree to go to war on a third major civ both have met. The proposer needs Foreign Trade. Either one may already be at war with the target (joining an ongoing war), but not both, and each one still at peace must be able to declare war. When the deal is accepted, each one not yet at war declares it with `CasusBelli::JointWar`: a formal war without the 5 turns of denouncement, with a formal war's grievances (100%). `CasusBelli::JointWar` is never held on its own (`hasCasusBelli` is false), so it only comes from a deal. Declaring war now goes through `Game::declareWarOn`, shared by the command and the deal. Deal value: +60 to a civ already fighting the target; otherwise +30 if it dislikes the target (opinion -20 or lower) and has at least 80% of its military strength, else -1000. AI: asks a civ it likes (opinion at the friend level, not distrusted) to join each war it fights. Unreal: the deal screen offers it ("a joint war on <civ>").

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- The war starts when the deal is accepted. The spec gives no delay, and the data's Duration 30 is the agreement's usual length.
- Not modelled: Sumeria's Adventures of Enkidu (Sumeria is not in the roster).
