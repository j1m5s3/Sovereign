# Record: the AI weighs Housing by the growth it gives back (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-09. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent, after a look at what holds the AI's cities back.

## What was wrong

- **AI cities lost over a third of their food to Housing caps.** In 8 AI games (Small, 6 AI) to turn 200, from turn 50 on about half the cities were within 1 Housing of their cap or over it on a given turn (a city grows at half pace with 1 Housing to spare, at a quarter from its cap to 4 over it), and 37-41% of their food surplus went to waste there. At turn 200, 39 of the 128 slowed cities could build a Granary and had none.
- **Production gave Housing a flat value,** 30 a point for a building and 40 for a district (6 points for any Aqueduct), once a city was within 2 of its cap, whatever its food. A Granary's 2 Housing (60) weighed less than a Library's 2 Science (250 at the Classical weights), so slowed cities built Campuses, Libraries and Universities first.

## Fix

- `production` (ai.cpp): Housing is worth the growth it gives back. Once the city's next citizen is in, its cap holds back a share of its food surplus (none with 2 Housing to spare, half with 1, three quarters from the cap to 4 over it, all of it beyond: `growthQuarters`, from the `CITY_HOUSING_LEFT_*` globals); the Housing of a building or district is worth the share it gives back, weighed as that much Food a turn, as its own Food is. A city with no food to spare gains nothing from it.
- An Aqueduct counts the Housing it gives that city, `Game::aqueductHousing`, now shared with the city report (2 by fresh water, else up to 6: 3 more on the coast, 4 inland); a Neighborhood or Preserve its own and 1 for Appeal (2 before, the most Appeal can add).

Tried and left out: weighing Housing by the room the city will have when it is built, with no citizen ahead for a city growing at a quarter pace or less (less of everything: science -2.0 ± 1.2, production -3.5 ± 1.7 over 128 seeds); settling by fresh water and the coast at 60 and 30 instead of 20 and 12 (on top of this, +1.2 ± 0.6 pop but -1.2 ± 1.4 science over 128 seeds); with that, looking two citizens ahead (less of everything), Housing weighed double, or Housing counted in Gold purchases (no better; 64 seeds each).

Speed (instructions, counted by Callgrind): the weighing costs next to nothing. A Small 6-AI game to turn 200 (seed 1) takes 13.17 billion against 13.90 on main, a different game by then (176 units at the end against 194), and 20 turns of a Huge 12-AI game from turn 250 6.53 against 6.54 billion.

Save and reload: 8 games in 5 setups, each saved and loaded once or twice, end on the straight game's state. The four replay games of the speed-up checks replay their own command logs; their end states move, as for any AI change.

## Tests

- `ai_builds_housing_where_growth_stalls`: a capital with six farmed neighbours (Housing 6) and a Campus picks between a Granary and a Library. At its cap it builds the Granary with 8 or 5 Food to spare (2 or 1.25 Food back), the Library with 4 (1 Food back); the Granary with room for 2 (its next citizen would halve its growth); the Library when starving or by fresh water (Housing 9). With Engineering an Aqueduct, 4 more Housing, comes first. On main the three Granary cases fail (it builds the Library).
- `lakes_feed_aqueducts_and_raise_appeal` also checks that a coastal city's Aqueduct adds 3 Housing (as on main; the AI now counts the same).
- A mutation check (18 small changes to the new code) fails the tests for 14. The four it misses: a city 5 or more over its cap counted as still growing (no city in the test games got there), no guard for starving cities (no choice in the tests turns on it), every Aqueduct counted at 6 (it differs only where the 2 it gives by fresh water leave the city's growth as it was) and the Appeal Housing of a Neighborhood or Preserve (untested).

## Left open

- Cities still lose about 30% of their food surplus to Housing after turn 50, most of it at their cap or with 1 Housing to spare. Much of the Housing they could build (an Aqueduct where a spot exists, a Preserve, a Lighthouse) is worth less to the AI than the science buildings beside it at its Science weights, and half of the slowed cities have no water at all.
- Builders weigh a farm's Housing as before (40 a point within 2 of the cap).
