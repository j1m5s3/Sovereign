## Milestone

1. **Done: quests** (`src/quests.cpp`; `GameState::quests`, save version 48): each living city-state keeps one open quest for every major civ that has seen its city and is not at war with it, drawn on a world turn from what that civ could do now: convert its city to the civ's religion, send it a trade route, clear a barbarian camp within 5 tiles of it, train a military unit or build a district its capital can produce, trigger an open Eureka or Inspiration, or recruit a great person of a class. Fulfilling it gives the civ an envoy there, and a new quest follows on a later world turn. Events fulfil the unit, district, Eureka, Inspiration, great person and camp quests; the conversion and trade route quests are checked each world turn. A camp cleared by someone else, or a city-state gone, ends the quest unrewarded. The HUD lists our open quests.
