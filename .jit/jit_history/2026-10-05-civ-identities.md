## Milestones (one PR each)

1. **Done: Unique units:** each civ's unit replaces its base unit for that civ only, built from the base unit's stats plus Sovereign's changes (strength, movement, cost, conditional bonuses, abilities).
2. **Done: Unique buildings and improvements:** the nine replacement buildings and the three unique improvements (Paradise Garden, Beacon Tower, Nilometer).
3. **Done: Civ abilities:** the twelve civ abilities, through civ-sourced modifiers and typed fields.
4. **Done: Leader abilities:** the twelve leader abilities likewise; Unreal's HUD names each civ's abilities and uniques.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Uniques are defined in `civilizations.json` as changes to a base unit, building or improvement, so they inherit everything else from the generated Civ data (unlocks, upgrades, costs) and stay in step with it.
- As built: `Json::overlay` lays a row with a `base` over its base row (units, buildings, improvements) while the rules load. Unique units and buildings carry `uniqueTo` and replace their base for that civ (`Rules::uniqueUnitFor`; `cityHasBuilding` makes a replacement satisfy prerequisites, unit requirements and modifiers written for its base; upgrades lead to the civ's unique through `Game::upgradeTarget`). Unit abilities reuse the ability/effect/condition model, with new effects (heal on kill, melee and ranged, capture as Builder) and conditions (adjacent same unit, opponent on a terrain climate); Builders from captures are spawned after the command so no unit pointer moves mid-combat. Buildings gained typed effects (a yield on adjacent improvements next to its district, gold per trade route, envoys when built, XP for units trained, food per adjacent mountain); improvements gained `uniqueTo`, amenities, defense, sight, border-only and river placement, flood shelter and a yield to adjacent improvements. Unlocks the roster left open: Paradise Garden at Early Empire, Beacon Tower at Construction, Nilometer at Irrigation.
- Civ and leader abilities: plot yields through civ-sourced modifiers (new requirement `PLOT_NEXT_TO_RIVER`), the rest through typed `CivAbility` fields (extra district adjacency, wonder and building production, amenities from wonders, governors, foreign religion and kills, founding bonuses, suzerainty culture, governor titles and yields, trade-route yields, farm housing, workable mountains, units' strength near the leader or near cities of the religion, granted abilities, builder charges, peace yields, Faith purchases). Each civ's two abilities are combined at load (`CivType::combined`, `Game::civAbility`) so hooks read one struct; `Player::killsThisEra` (save version 35).
- Approximations, listed in `civilizations.json`'s comment: road Movement for Cyrus, Pachacuti and the Chasqui and Qin's free roads are not modelled (Builders do not lay roads); the War Chariot and Chasqui get +1 Movement everywhere; the Mandinka Lancer has its +5 in desert but not the desert Movement; the Legionary's Builder charge builds any improvement; the Repeating Crossbow may also move after attacking; the Calmecac gives units trained there a quarter of their first promotion's XP; Egypt's floodplain placement already had no penalty.
- Benchmark (Small, 4 civs, seeds 1-5) at turn 300: techs 37.5 -> 40.9, science 110 -> 127; 450-turn soaks run cleanly.
