## Milestones

1. **Done: slotting Dark Age cards:** the generator turns "Dark Age X Era-Y Era" into `darkAge` and an `eras` window (`PolicyType::darkAge`, `minEra`, `maxEra`). A card is available only in a Dark Age while the world era is in its window. It goes in a Wildcard slot, under the usual rules for changing policies. When the world enters a new era, cards that are no longer available (a new age, or past their window) leave their slots. The effects that fit the modifier model are in `modifiers.json`. Monasticism: +75% Science in cities with a Holy Site (new requirement `CITY_HAS_DISTRICT`), -25% Culture. Robber Barons: +25% Production with a Factory, +50% Gold with a Stock Exchange, -2 Amenities. Collectivism: +1 Food on Farms, +2 Housing, +100% Industrial Zone adjacency, -50% great person points. Elite Forces: +100% XP, +2 Gold upkeep per unit. Inquisition: -25% Science and the friendly-territory ability. Disinformation Campaign: -10% Science and Culture. Automated Workforce: -1 Amenity, -5 Loyalty. Letters of Marque: +100% Production toward naval raiders. Twilight Valor and Cyber Warfare: their abilities. The generator now reads "+N Combat Strength in friendly territory" as an own-territory bonus, and theological combat counts such bonuses (the Inquisitor's +35 too). AI: a Dark Age card's negative modifiers count against it.
2. **Done: the effects in code** (`Game::policyIs`): Rogue State (+50% toward the Manhattan Project, Operation Ivy and nuclear devices; no envoys from buildings, influence, civics, villages or quests), Automated Workforce (+20% toward projects), Isolationism (no new cities or Settlers; domestic routes +2 Food and +2 Production), Twilight Valor (units do not heal), Cyber Warfare (grievances against the civ do not decay; its +10 applies against Information-era units through the new combat condition `OPPONENT_MIN_ERA`), Disinformation Campaign (+3 Favor per Broadcast Center), Letters of Marque (naval raiders +2 Movement, +100% plunder for all units through `Game::plunderPercent`, trade route yields halved), Flower Power (units can only be bought, at twice the Gold price, except Rock Bands; +50% concert tourism while at peace with everyone).

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- The window is the world era (`GameState::gameEra`), as for dedications.
- Flower Power's data reads "100% cheaper to purchase Rock Band" and "-100% cheaper to purchase All units". Taken together, Sovereign reads them as Rock Bands at their usual price and other units at double. "-100% Production toward all units" is read as no training by production.
- Collectivism's "+100% to the district's Production (adjacency) yield" is read as the Industrial Zone's adjacency, the one district whose adjacency yields Production.
- Not modelled: Inquisition's +1 Inquisitor charge (Inquisitors are not yet bought; `faithPurchaseCost`), and Monasticism's science on a Holy Site that is pillaged (it still counts while complete).
