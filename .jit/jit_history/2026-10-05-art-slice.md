## Decisions (do not reopen)
- **Source of truth is code.** Every model is generated by a Python script in `tools/art/blender/` (bmesh, deterministic). No hand-edited `.blend` files are committed.
- **Pipeline:**
  - `tools/art/build_art.py` runs Blender headless to generate and export each piece as FBX into `art/build/` (git-ignored).
  - It then runs the Unreal editor with `tools/art/ue_import.py`, which imports the FBX into `/Game/Art/<Kit>/` and creates the master material.
  - The generated `.uasset` files in `unreal/Content/Art/` are committed (small, binary in `.gitattributes`), so a fresh clone runs with art. Rerunning the pipeline regenerates them.
- **Look (stylized realism, simplified):** real proportions; flat-shaded planes with vertex colours painted by script (base colour, darker bases and edges, a light top gradient standing in for hand-painted shading).
  - One master material, `M_SovKit`: vertex colour × `Tint`. Per-instance tint carries owner colours.
  - No downloaded geometry or textures ship (the doc's licence rule).
- **Scale:** 1 unit = 1 cm. Street pieces at street scale (a house ~5 m); the map uses the same meshes scaled down.
- **Kits for this slice:**
  - `Nature`: broadleaf tree, conifer, bush, rocks.
  - `Classical` (temperate City Center): three houses, Palace, Monument, Granary, temple, generic landmark, wall segment, market stall, boarded house, banner.
  - `Figures`: citizen, soldier (spear and shield), leader (crown and cloak), herald, captain. Static figures, owner colour via `Tint`.
- **Use in game:** street, battle and map scenes pick meshes by recipe through one art library that loads by path and falls back to primitives when an asset is missing:
  - landmark recipes by building id;
  - houses by seed;
  - trees on woods plots;
  - figures for units, citizens and soldiers.
- **Tests:** an automation test checks that every kit asset the code names exists and loads. Screenshots on James's PC verify the look.
