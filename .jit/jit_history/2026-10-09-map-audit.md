# Record: map and terrain rules the spec audit found off (01)

Status: done, 2026-10-09. Previous: `2026-10-09-units-audit.md`. A read-only audit compared specs/civ6/01-map-and-terrain.md with the core, leaving out what `2026-10-06-spec-audit-gaps.md`, `2026-10-06-spec-audit-part-2.md`, `2026-10-09-city-rules.md`, `2026-10-09-units-audit.md` and the decisions already settle, and confirmed 5 rules that differ in code; this record covers 4. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent.

## Fixed

- **Barbarians count major civs only** (01: Barbarians, "at most 3 camps per major civ", "technology known by the majors"): the camp target, the plots kept out of sight and the tech half the civs must know (`BARBARIAN_TECH_PERCENT`) all counted city-states and Free Cities too, so a Standard map with 12 city-states aimed for 60 camps instead of 24, and barbarian units lagged behind the majors' tech (`Game::placeCamps`, `Game::campUnitType`; `Game::isMajorCiv`).
- **A harvest can take a bonus resource under a feature** (01: Harvest): a plot whose feature can be removed is harvested for the feature, as before, unless the command asks for the resource (`Command::harvest(p, u, true)`, arg 1), which leaves the feature (the Bananas in a Rainforest, the Deer in Woods). A plot whose feature cannot be removed (Floodplains, Reef) is harvested for its resource; before, it could not be harvested at all (`Game::canHarvestAt`).
- **A natural wonder's adjacent yields count once** (01: Natural wonders, "adjacent tiles"): a plot touching two plots of one wonder got its yields twice, and Torres del Paine tripled the terrain's yields instead of doubling them (`Game::plotYields`).
- **A pillaged district beside a plot gives -1 Appeal** (01: Appeal, "pillaged tiles"), as a pillaged improvement does, instead of its own Appeal (`Game::plotAppeal`).

Results:
- 128 AI games (Small, 6 AI, turn 200) against main: with fewer barbarian camps the AI civs grow faster: science 129.7 to 135.5 (+5.8 ± 1.3), culture +3.3 ± 0.8, production +6.0 ± 1.7, population +1.6, cities 8.8 to 9.0, techs +0.8.
- 8 long AI games in 8 setups (up to Huge, 400 turns): no crash or replay mismatch; all 8 play out differently.
- Tests: `barbarian_camps_count_only_the_major_civs`, `a_city_states_sight_does_not_keep_camps_away`, `barbarian_units_follow_the_major_civs_tech`, `builder_harvests_a_resource_under_a_feature`, the two-plot wonder and Torres del Paine in `a_natural_wonder_feeds_its_neighbours_and_blocks_building`, the pillaged Holy Site in `districts_wonders_and_the_biosphere_beside_a_plot_change_its_appeal`. Mutation check: 10 mutants, all caught (a plot only a city-state sees, `a_city_states_sight_does_not_keep_camps_away`, and a Harvest command with arg 2 on Floodplains were added to catch the last two).

## Left open

- **No Volcanoes or Geothermal Fissures on generated maps** (01: features; 09: eruptions). Map generation places neither, so the three generic eruptions never find a site, and the Geothermal Plant, the Campus and Aqueduct bonuses beside a fissure and the Goddess of Fire's fissure Faith never apply; only the volcano natural wonders erupt. The spec gives no density, so placing them needs a Sovereign reading.
- The Unreal harvest key still sends the default (the feature first); taking the resource from a plot with a removable feature needs a second key there.
- Unsure, not changed: Pamukkale's "adjacent districts gain extra yields" (no data rows), Lysefjord topping XP up every turn, and a camp beside a lake becoming a naval tribe.
