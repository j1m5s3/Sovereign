# Plan: Levy Military (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-06. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-06-building-effects.md`. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent. A suzerain's Levy Military from 08 was missing, and the Foreign Ministry waited on it. One milestone, one PR.

Specs: 08-diplomacy-city-states-governors (Suzerain: "can Levy Military: pay 25% of the units' purchase cost to control its units for 30 turns"); data/global-parameters (LEVY_MILITARY_*); data/buildings (Foreign Ministry).

## Milestone

1. **Done:** `Command::levyMilitary` (`CommandType::LevyMilitary` = 65).
   - The suzerain of a living city-state it is at peace with pays `levyCost`. That is LEVY_MILITARY_PERCENT_OF_UNIT_PURCHASE_COST (25%) of each of the city-state's military units' Gold purchase price, halved with a Foreign Ministry.
   - Those units become its own until LEVY_MILITARY_TURN_DURATION (30, speed-scaled) runs out (`GameState::levies`, save version 67).
   - At the start of the suzerain's turn, an expired levy sends the surviving units home. They are disbanded if the city-state is gone. One levy per city-state at a time.
   - Foreign Ministry: levied units +4 strength (`Game::levied`).
   - AI: levies at war when it has the Gold and 100 more.
   - Unreal: the city-state chooser (O) offers the levy with its cost.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Levied units cost their new owner's upkeep while they serve. The spec does not say otherwise.
- The levy outlasts a lost suzerainty. Only time ends it.
