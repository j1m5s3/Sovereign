# Plan: the late game: power, carriers and airstrips, nuclear weapons (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-05. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-05-civ-identities.md`. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent: the remaining Civ VI late-game systems the specs describe and the core lacks. James can redirect at any point (his art and UI review is open whenever he wants it).

Specs: 09-civs-eras-victory-climate (Power [GS]); data/buildings (Power [GS], Extra when powered) and data/improvements (renewables); 05-units-and-combat (Aircraft Carrier, Airstrip, WMDs) with data/units and data/projects (Manhattan Project, devices).

## Milestones (one PR each)

1. **Done: Power:** buildings that need power (Factory, Stock Exchange, Research Lab, Stadium...) and their bonus when fully powered; a city short of power loses up to 50% production. Sources: Coal, Oil and Nuclear Power Plants burn their resource for 4/4/16 power to every own city within 6 tiles (replacing today's flat burn, so CO2 follows real demand), the Hydroelectric Dam (+6), and the renewables (Solar, Wind and Offshore Wind Farms +2, Geothermal Plant +4). AI builds plants where demand goes unmet; the HUD shows a city's power.
2. **Done: Carriers and airstrips:** the Aircraft Carrier carries aircraft (2 slots) and the Airstrip improvement (3 slots, Military Engineers) gives aircraft a base in the field.
3. **Done: Nuclear weapons:** the Manhattan Project and Operation Ivy, building Nuclear and Thermonuclear Devices, delivery by bombers, missile silos or nuclear submarines, blast damage and fallout; the AI keeps them as deterrence and answers in kind.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Milestone 1 as built: generated `requiredPower`, `poweredYields`, `poweredAmenities`, `burns` (resource and power per unit: Coal 4, Oil 4, Uranium 16) and `powerProvided` (Hydroelectric Dam 6; Solar, Wind and Offshore Wind Farms 2, Geothermal Plant 4) on buildings and improvements. `Game::burnPower` (start of the civ's turn) sets each city's `powerDemand` and `powerSupply` (save version 37): free sources first, then the civ's plants within 6 tiles burn just enough stockpile, nearest first, with their CO2 (so CO2 now follows real demand, replacing the flat burn of one fuel per plant). A fully powered city gets its buildings' powered yields and amenities; a short one loses production up to POWER_MAX_PRODUCTION_MODIFIER_PENALTY (-50%) in proportion to the shortfall. Not modelled: the Terrestrial Laser Station's demand. Decommissioning, converting and nuclear accidents followed (`2026-10-05-competitions.md` follow-up), as did Cardiff's suzerain power (+2 per Lighthouse, Shipyard and Seaport) and Synthetic Technocracy (+3 Power per city, +30% toward projects, -10% Tourism); Reyna's Renewable Subsidizer already gives its Gold. AI: plants valued by the shortfall within reach when fuel is on hand, power-hungry buildings discounted where no power will come, Builders lay renewables in short cities, Carbon Recapture only once the world is warming. Unreal: the city line shows power. A Deity all-AI game now reaches climate phase I (+0.5 degrees) before turn 400.
- Milestone 2 as built: the generator now takes the Military Engineer's improvements (Fort, defense +4; Airstrip, 3 air slots; Missile Silo, for milestone 3) with `builtBy`, and only that unit builds them (Builders the rest); the Mountain Tunnel waits for mountain movement. The Aircraft Carrier carries 2 aircraft (no promotions for more yet). `Game::airSlots` adds the player's carriers on a plot and an owned, unpillaged Airstrip to any city or Aerodrome slots; a carrier moving takes up to its slots of the owner's aircraft with it; after every command, aircraft beyond their base's slots (lowest ids keep theirs) are lost, so a sunk carrier or a lost Airstrip takes its aircraft down. AI: Builders skip engineer improvements; the AI does not yet build Airstrips or base aircraft on carriers. Unreal: the build chooser lists only what the selected unit can build.
- Milestone 3 as built: the Manhattan Project and Operation Ivy are modelled (they only unlock the device projects); Build Nuclear/Thermonuclear Device spends its Uranium and adds a device (`Player::wmds`, save version 38) costing 14/16 Gold a turn. The weapons table (blast radius 1/2, fallout 10/20 turns, ICBM range 12/15) is generated into `wmds` in projects.json; units get `deliversWmd` (Bomber, Jet Bomber, Nuclear Submarine) and `wmdImmune` (Giant Death Robot). `LaunchWmd` (command 51): a bomber within its strike range, a Nuclear Submarine or the player's unpillaged Missile Silo within the ICBM range, never with a plot or unit of a civ at peace with the launcher in the blast. The blast destroys units (a leader survives at 10 HP: Sovereign), drops walls to 0 and the city center to 1 HP, pillages improvements for the fallout turns, takes a citizen from a city for each of its plots covered (at least 1 left), and contaminates the plots: not worked, and units on them lose PLOT_CONTAMINATION_DAMAGE_BASE (50) each world turn (`processFallout`). Victims remember it (-40 for 100 turns) with 300 grievances; every other civ that has met the launcher remembers it (-15). Not modelled: war weariness (no war weariness system yet), the nuclear emergency [GS], Rogue State and Arms Race Proponent, districts and buildings pillaged by the blast, launching from the Unreal map by silo. AI: Manhattan/Ivy valued 150 (300 when going for Domination), keeps two devices while it can afford them, never declares war on a nuclear power while it holds none, and strikes back only at a civ that has hit it with one (the biggest city in reach, strongest device first, once a turn). Unreal: Ctrl+right-click with a bomber or Nuclear Submarine delivers the strongest device held; the HUD lists every nuclear arsenal.
- Power is computed for each civ at the start of its turn: local sources first, then the nearest plant cities burn just enough of their stockpile to cover each remaining shortfall.
