# Plan: policy card effects (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-06. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-06-dark-age-policies.md`. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent: of the 140 policy cards, about 110 can be slotted but do nothing. Only the Ancient and Classical cards and the Dark Age cards have hand-written modifiers. James can redirect at any point.

Specs: 04-tech-civics-government (Policies); data/governments-policies (every card's effect text).

## Milestones (one PR each)

1. **Done: Generated policy modifiers.** The generator reads each card's effect text into modifiers (`policies.json`, per card `modifiers`; text it cannot read stays in `untrackedEffects`). It skips cards that already have hand-written modifiers. The core loads them like `modifiers.json`. This milestone covers the forms the modifier model already has, plus the requirements they need:
   - unit production by class and era (new `minEra` filter) or by unit;
   - combat XP; unit upkeep; district adjacency; strength against barbarians;
   - yields from a building; flat and percent city yields;
   - amenities, housing, loyalty and growth, with `where` conditions (new requirements: `CITY_HAS_GARRISON`, `CITY_MIN_SPECIALTY_DISTRICTS`, `CITY_HAS_GOVERNOR`);
   - granted abilities; builder charges; war weariness; plot cost; yield per citizen.
2. **Done: New effect kinds:** trade route yields (all, domestic, international, to allies and city-states), production toward wonders by era, specific buildings and districts and their buildings, great person points (player and per building), Diplomatic Favor and influence points per turn.
3. **Done: Military and economy cards in code:** Bastions; Logistics and Integrated Attack Logistics; National Identity; Native Conquest; upgrade discounts (Professional Army, Retinues, Force Modernization); Raid and Total War; Wars of Religion; resource accumulation (Drill Manuals, Equestrian Orders, Resource Management); Free Market, Grand Opera, Rationalism, Simultaneum; Public Transport; Market Economy's goods at the destination; Aerospace Contractors.
4. **Done: Diplomacy, espionage and tourism cards in code:** Heritage Tourism, Satellite Broadcasts, Online Communities, Space Tourism; spy cards (Cryptography, Machiavellianism, Nuclear Espionage, Non-State Actors); envoy cards (Containment, Diplomatic League, Merchant Confederation, Gunboat Diplomacy); per-suzerainty yields (Collective Activism, International Space Agency, Raj); Communications Office; Rabblerousing; Hallyu; Music Censorship.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Specialty districts are those that count toward the population limit (`DistrictType::needsPopulation`).
- Milestone 1 as built: 117 modifiers from 60 cards. Unit production by class and era is merged into one modifier for each unbroken run of eras. The data's replacement cards (Chivalry, Grande Armée...) also list the earlier eras, so they cover them. `CITY_HAS_GOVERNOR` with a value counts the governor's titles (`promotions.size()`). AI: a card's modifiers count up to five, since one line of text can become many modifiers.
- Milestone 2 as built: 182 modifiers in all. New effects: `ADJUST_TRADE_ROUTE_YIELD` (scope ALL, DOMESTIC, INTERNATIONAL, ALLY, CITY_STATE, SUZERAIN; on the origin's yields, `tradeRouteModifierYields`); `ADJUST_ITEM_PRODUCTION_PERCENT` (WONDERS by the wonder's unlock era, BUILDING, DISTRICT, DISTRICT_BUILDINGS, SPACE_RACE; applied after the item's own bonuses, so it multiplies them); `ADJUST_GREAT_PERSON_POINTS` and `ADJUST_CITY_GREAT_PERSON_POINTS`; `ADJUST_FAVOR_PER_TURN` and `ADJUST_CITY_FAVOR_PER_TURN`; `ADJUST_INFLUENCE_PER_TURN`. Isolationism's domestic route bonus is now one of these generated modifiers, replacing its code. Policy text naming a civ's unique building or district (Tsikhe) is left untracked, since unique rows are not generated. Not modelled: route yields "to destination" (the partner's share: Wisselbanken, Democratic Legacy; since added in `2026-10-06-alliance-effects.md`) and alliance points per turn.
- Milestone 3 as built (`Game::policyIs` hooks). Bastions: +6 city strength and +5 to city strikes. Logistics and Integrated Attack Logistics: +1 Movement for units starting the turn in their own territory, or in an enemy's. National Identity halves the strength lost to wounds. Native Conquest: Gold equal to half a killed unit's strength. Upgrades: half the Gold (Professional Army, Force Modernization) and half the strategic resources, rounded up (Retinues, Force Modernization; `upgradeResourceCost`). Raid and Total War: +50% plunder (`plunderPercent`). Wars of Religion: +4 against units of a civ of another religion (`civReligion`: the one it founded, else its capital's), not for religious units. The resource cards: +1 a turn for each improved source, read like Victor's Defense Logistics. The four district-building cards: +50% of the district's yield from its buildings with an adjacency of 4 or more for it, and +50% more in a city of 15 or more. Public Transport: 100 Gold for each new Neighborhood; a Neighborhood gives +1 Gold, +3 Food and +1 Production at appeal 2 or more, and +1 Food and +1 Production more at 4 or more. Market Economy: +1 Gold on international routes for each improved luxury or strategic resource of the destination city. Aerospace Contractors: +3 power and +3 Aluminum a turn in cities with a Spaceport. Not modelled: Second Strike Capability ("+50% nuclear weapon maintenance" reads as a cost with no gain).
- Milestone 4 as built. Heritage Tourism doubles the tourism of sculptures, portraits, landscapes, religious works and artifacts; Satellite Broadcasts triples music. Online Communities: +50% more tourism toward civs we run a route to. Space Tourism is read as protective: tourism toward its holder is 20% lower. Cryptography: our spies work a level higher abroad, and foreign spies two levels lower in our cities. Machiavellianism: offensive operations 25% faster. Nuclear Espionage: one more boost from Steal Tech Boost. Diplomatic League: the first envoy to a city-state counts twice. Containment: +1 envoy where a rival is suzerain. Merchant Confederation: +1 Gold per envoy placed. Collective Activism and International Space Agency: +5% Culture or Science in every city per suzerainty (`suzeraintiesOf`). Raj: +2 Gold, Faith, Science and Culture in the capital per suzerainty. Communications Office: +1 loyalty a turn per title of the city's own established governor. Rabblerousing: half the favor spent on extra votes for the outcome that passed comes back. Music Censorship: foreign Rock Bands cannot enter. No-ops in Sovereign: Non-State Actors and Hallyu ("may choose any promotion"; spies already may, and bands draw from all their promotions), and Gunboat Diplomacy's open borders (city-states' borders are already open; its +4 influence is generated).
