# Record: civ abilities and uniques the audit found off

Status: done, 2026-10-09. An audit of `specs/sovereign/leaders-and-art-style.md` (Leader pool; Civ abilities, uniques and dynasties) against the core found 5 gaps. This record fixes 4; the fifth is noted below as unsure. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent.

## Change

- **The starting leader holds its leaning's first promotion** (doc: "the leader starts with the first promotion of one SOVEREIGN branch (Warlord, Statesman or Builder-King)"). Before, each civ's `leaning` was read and never used, and every leader began with no promotion. `Rules::leaningPromotion(civ)` finds the tier-1 SOVEREIGN promotion of the branch the leaning names ("Builder-King" names `BUILDER_KING`), and `Game::create` gives it to each starting leader. A successor keeps bringing its own promotions (`Game::successorPromotions`), so this is for the first leader only. The leader starts at level 2 as a result.
- **Builder of Monuments reaches light cavalry and chariots only** (doc: "light cavalry and chariots +5 Combat Strength on flat desert and floodplains"). Before, the ability named the whole `HEAVY_CAVALRY` class, so Egypt's Knights, Cuirassiers and Tanks got it too. It now names `LIGHT_CAVALRY` and a `CHARIOT` tag carried by the War Chariot (Egypt's replacement for the Heavy Chariot, so Egypt trains no other chariot).
- **Gift of the Nile spares Egypt's floodplain districts** (doc: "districts and wonders can be built on floodplains with no penalty"). Sovereign reading: the penalty for building there is flood damage, so floods no longer pillage Egypt's districts or idle their buildings (`CivAbility::floodSafeDistricts`). Improvements, units and population still suffer, and other disasters (storms) still wreck districts. The flood's random draw is still made, so other civs' games are unchanged.
- **Arabia's desert route gold looks at the way the route takes** (doc and data: routes whose way crosses desert +2 Gold). Before, it checked the straight line between the cities. A running route now passes its stored plots (`TradeRoute::path`) to `Game::tradeRouteYields(origin, destination, way)`; without a way (an AI or the UI choosing a destination) the straight line remains the estimate.

## Checks

- Pace (128 AI games, 6 civs on Small maps to turn 200, against #287): the games change, since every leader now starts promoted (a Builder-King's Overseer and a Statesman's Wary act from turn 1). Averages at turn 200 move slightly up and within noise for most measures: science +3.1 (t 2.6), culture +1.7 (t 2.2), population +0.6, cities unchanged.
- Soak (8 long AI games with save/reload cuts, to turns 300-400): no crash, replay mismatch or reload drift; all 8 end states differ from #287. Time per game unchanged (two alternating pairs).
- Tests: `a_starting_leader_holds_its_leanings_first_promotion`, `builder_of_monuments_favours_light_cavalry_and_chariots`, `gift_of_the_nile_spares_floodplain_districts`, `arabias_desert_routes_follow_the_way_taken`. `ai_soak_takes_a_capital_and_replays` moved to seed 71, as its old seed's game no longer took a capital by turn 250 once leaders start promoted (it has moved seed after other rule changes before). Mutation: 13 mutants, 11 caught. Missed: `t.tier >= 1` in place of `t.tier == 1` (each branch's tier-1 promotion comes first in `leader.json`, so the first match is the same), and dropping the upper bound check on the civ index (reading past the table is undefined behaviour no plain test can see).

## Unsure

- The doc says China's Repeating Crossbow (Crossbowman unique) "can attack after moving", but every Crossbowman already can here (only siege units set up first); the unique also lets it move after attacking, which is what sets it apart. Code left as is; the doc's line may want rewording.
