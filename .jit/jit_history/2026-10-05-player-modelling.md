## Milestones (one PR each)

1. **Done (with 2): The profile, built from play:** a `PlayerProfile` per human in the game state, updated each world turn from what that player does (deterministic and saved, so replays match): army composition by promotion class, aggression (wars declared, cities taken, armies massed near AI borders), tech and civic leaning (military, economy, culture, faith), expansion pace, and how exposed the leader is (turns outside a city, unescorted, near enemies). Values fade slowly so recent play counts most.
2. **Done (with 1): Countering what the AI sees:** production and research lean to counters (anti-cavalry against cavalry players, ranged and walls against melee rushes, siege against walled turtles, navies against naval players), defence and garrisons against aggressive players, assassins sent at a leader that roams exposed, wariness in diplomacy toward a habitual backstabber. Difficulty gates it: Settler and Chieftain ignore the profile, Warlord to Prince use the army mix only, King and up use all of it.
3. **Done: Live-battle habits:** the battle simulation measures what the human does in a live battle (flanking share, retreats, charging with the leader, focus on ranged units) and reports it with the battle result; the profile keeps it and the battle AI adapts within its existing styles (reserves on the flanks against flankers, screens for its ranged units, targeting an exposed leader), without retraining mid-game.
4. **Done (this PR): The profile across games:** exported at the end of a game (and on save) to a small file per player name and fed back at the start of the next game through the setup, so it enters the game as data and replays still match; Unreal reads and writes it in the user's save folder; tests that a loaded profile shapes the AI from turn 1.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Milestones 1-2 as built: `PlayerProfile` per player in `GameState::profiles` (save version 30), updated in the world turn by `Game::processProfiles` (`src/profile.cpp`) for every major civ, human or AI (the AI counters AIs too; only humans' profiles will be carried between games). Fields x1000 fading 1/20 per turn toward the sample: army share by class (melee, ranged, anti-cavalry, light and heavy cavalry, siege, naval, other; `profileClassOf`), militarism (strength per city against the world), expansion (cities against the average), science/culture/faith shares, aggression (a 5000 spike per war declared plus the share of its army camped within 4 tiles of a peaceful major's city), leader outside its cities and exposed to assassins; counts of wars and surprise wars declared and conquered cities held. Counters in the AI's posture: neighbours' (cities within 14 tiles, or at war) army mix weighted by their strength chooses a favoured class (35% cavalry: anti-cavalry; 50% melee: ranged; 40% ranged: light cavalry), +1200 in its unit choice; from King: neighbours that declared surprise wars, run aggression 300+ or militarism 1.5x get +30% army and +150 wall value, surprise-war makers are offered no friendship or open borders, siege-heavy neighbours +100 walls, an exposed enemy leader +150 assassin value. Settler and Chieftain ignore profiles, Warlord to Prince use the army mix.
- Milestone 3 as built: `battle::Sim::habits(side)` measures shares x1000 of a side's squad time under flank, fall-back and hunt-leader orders and of the battle its leader spent within two reaches of an enemy; Unreal sends the human side's four numbers as the `BattleResult` command's data (validated 0..1000, optional); `PlayerProfile` keeps them (`battles`, `battleFlank`, `battleFallBack`, `battleHunt`, `battleLeaderFront`; each battle moves them a quarter of the way; save version 31). `battle::Counter` (`Commander::setCounter`, `applyCounter`) adjusts the commander's orders without touching the network: hold the side squads against a flanker (flank share 300+), the centre hunts a leader who fights in front (400+), no holding or falling back against a retreater (fall-back 250+); Unreal sets it from the human's profile at King and up. Remote (online guest) views send no habits, since the host runs the scene.
- Milestone 4 as built: `PlayerSetup` carries `hasProfile` and a `PlayerProfile` (save version 32); `Game::create` seeds the game's profiles from it (conquered cities reset to zero). `profileToText` / `profileFromText` give a small versioned text file ("sovereign-profile 1", then "key value" lines; unknown keys skipped so newer files load in older builds). Unreal: the local human's profile is read from `Saved/Sovereign/Profiles/<name>.txt` when a local or hosted game starts and written on every save (autosaves run each turn). Not yet: joining players' profiles (they would have to send theirs to the host in the lobby).
- The profile is game state, not hidden AI memory: it updates in the world turn from public facts, so every machine in an online game computes the same profile and replays reproduce it. A profile carried between games enters through `GameSetup`, like any other start option.
