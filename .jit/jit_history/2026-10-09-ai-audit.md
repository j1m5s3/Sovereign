# Record: AI spec audit, part 1 (opinion inputs)

Status: done, 2026-10-09. An audit of the AI sections of `specs/civ6/10-ai-ui-implementation.md` against the core found 13 gaps (listed below). This part adds the opinion inputs the diplomacy layer was missing. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent.

## Change

- **Opinion counts shared enemies, crowded borders and a fixed random part** (10, diplomacy layer: "opinion score per civ from modifiers (agendas, shared enemies, borders/proximity, grievances, deals, religion, government, and the difficulty-scaled random offset)"). `Game::opinionReasons` gains three reasons:
  - `SharedEnemy`: both at war with the same major civ, +`OPINION_SHARED_ENEMY` (4).
  - `NearBorder`: at peace and not allied, with a city of theirs within `OPINION_NEAR_BORDER_RANGE` (6) plots of one of ours, `OPINION_NEAR_BORDER` (-3). Civ VI's near-border warning, read through city distance so it stays cheap.
  - `Disposition`: a fixed random part for each pair in a game, -`OPINION_RANDOM_RANGE`..+`OPINION_RANDOM_RANGE` (2) from the game's seed (`Game::dispositionRoll`), plus, from an AI toward a human, the difficulty's `diplomacyOffset` (Civ's DifficultyOffset on STANDARD_DIPLOMACY_RANDOM, 00: Settler +3 .. Prince 0 .. Deity -4).
- The values are Sovereign tuning (the game data gives the modifiers but not their sizes); they live in `data/rules/setup.json` globals.

## Checks

- Pace (128 paired seeds, Small, 6 AI, turn 200, the new reasons zeroed vs on): within noise. Before #303: cities +0.09 ± 0.05, science -0.9 ± 0.8, production +1.5 ± 1.2. On #303: cities +0.06 ± 0.04, science -1.2 ± 0.9, production +0.2 ± 1.1, Gold +11 ± 5.
- Soak (8 long games with save/reload cuts): no crash, replay mismatch or reload drift. Timing about the same (11.4s vs 11.1s for the four timed games, within run-to-run spread).
- Test `opinion_counts_shared_enemies_crowded_borders_and_disposition`; the diplomacy tests now pick a seed whose random parts are all 0, so they see only the reasons they set up. Mutation: 9 mutants, 8 caught; the one missed (dropping "not the holder" from the shared-enemy scan) is equivalent, since no civ is at war with itself.

## The audit's other findings (for later parts)

1. The AI builds and moves no navy (Naval strategy, naval superiority operation). Large.
2. Settlers travel without escorts: measured before and not worth an escort's tempo (memory note on AI ideas dropped).
3. No boost chasing, nor its Warlord gate. Medium.
4. The AI_ tuning constants in globals are never read: one 10-turn gap covers trade, friendship and peace offers (spec: 10, 5, 3). Small.
5. Envoys ignore strategy and city-state type. Small.
6. Missionaries go to the nearest city, not low-pressure high-population ones; religious spending ignores strategy. Small.
7. No Early Exploration strategy. Small.
8. District placement weighs only current adjacency. Medium.
9. Promotions take the first offered. Small.
10. Settle scoring differs from StandardSettlePlot (no strategic bonuses, continent penalty or decay); plan A found it is not what limits expansion. Small.
11. A call to arms declares war but sends no help. Medium.
12. No timed autosave or per-turn statistics for replay graphs. Medium.
