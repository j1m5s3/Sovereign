# Record: AI spec audit (opinion inputs, offer pacing, envoys, missionaries, promotions)

Status: done, 2026-10-09. An audit of the AI sections of `specs/civ6/10-ai-ui-implementation.md` against the core found 13 gaps (listed below). This part adds the opinion inputs the diplomacy layer was missing. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent.

## Change

- **Opinion counts shared enemies, crowded borders and a fixed random part** (10, diplomacy layer: "opinion score per civ from modifiers (agendas, shared enemies, borders/proximity, grievances, deals, religion, government, and the difficulty-scaled random offset)"). `Game::opinionReasons` gains three reasons:
  - `SharedEnemy`: both at war with the same major civ, +`OPINION_SHARED_ENEMY` (4).
  - `NearBorder`: at peace and not allied, with a city of theirs within `OPINION_NEAR_BORDER_RANGE` (6) plots of one of ours, `OPINION_NEAR_BORDER` (-3). Civ VI's near-border warning, read through city distance so it stays cheap.
  - `Disposition`: a fixed random part for each pair in a game, -`OPINION_RANDOM_RANGE`..+`OPINION_RANDOM_RANGE` (2) from the game's seed (`Game::dispositionRoll`), plus, from an AI toward a human, the difficulty's `diplomacyOffset` (Civ's DifficultyOffset on STANDARD_DIPLOMACY_RANDOM, 00: Settler +3 .. Prince 0 .. Deity -4).
- The values are Sovereign tuning (the game data gives the modifiers but not their sizes); they live in `data/rules/setup.json` globals.

## Checks

- Pace (128 paired seeds, Small, 6 AI, turn 200, the new reasons zeroed vs on): within noise. Before #303: cities +0.09 ± 0.05, science -0.9 ± 0.8, production +1.5 ± 1.2. On #303: cities +0.06 ± 0.04, science -1.2 ± 0.9, production +0.2 ± 1.1, Gold +11 ± 5.
- Soak (8 long games with save/reload cuts): no crash, replay mismatch or reload drift. Timing about the same (11.4s vs 11.1s for the four timed games, within run-to-run spread).
- Test `opinion_counts_shared_enemies_crowded_borders_and_disposition`; the diplomacy tests now pick a seed whose random parts are all 0, so they see only the reasons they set up. Mutation: 9 mutants, 8 caught; the one missed (dropping "not the holder" from the shared-enemy scan) is equivalent, since no civ is at war with itself.

## Part 2: offers paced by kind

- **Friendship, peace and other deals on their own pace** (10, AI tuning constants: "trade offered at most every 10 turns, friendship offers every 5, peace offers every 3"). Before, one 10-turn gap (`kProposalGap`) covered every proposal to a civ, and the `AI_TURNS_BETWEEN_*` globals were never read. Now a city offered for peace waits `AI_TURNS_BETWEEN_PEACE_OFFERS` (3) turns after the last proposal, a friendship or alliance `AI_TURNS_BETWEEN_FRIENDSHIP_OFFERS` (5), and any other deal `AI_TURNS_BETWEEN_TRADES` (10). `kProposalGap` remains only as the alliance renewal window.
- Checks: pace within noise (128 paired seeds; science +0.2 ± 0.2, every game but 12 the same; AI deals with other AIs are made only when accepted, so the pace mostly binds offers to humans). Test `ai_offers_are_paced_by_kind`, and `an_ai_losing_badly_offers_a_city_for_peace` now expects the 3-turn gap. Mutation: 4 mutants, all caught.
- The other AI_ constants (base luxury value, aid value, ZOC discount, task bias, island coast share) still go unread; the AI's own valuations cover them.

## Part 3: envoys follow strategy

- **Envoys toward the city-states our strategy wants** (10, AI behaviour: "envoy allocation to reach thresholds/suzerainty aligned with strategy"). Before, envoys went by tier only, ties to the first city-state in turn order. Now a city-state whose kind the AI's strategy wants scores 12 more (`kEnvoyStrategyBonus` in `ai.cpp`, short of a tier's 20, so a tier within reach still comes first): Scientific for a Science victory, Cultural for Culture, Religious for Religion, Militaristic for Domination or a war with a major civ, Industrial for a wonder-obsessed AI.
- Checks: pace within noise (128 paired seeds; science +0.7 ± 0.4, gold -4.7 ± 3.6). Test `ai_sends_envoys_to_the_city_states_its_strategy_wants` (at war the envoy goes to the Militaristic city-state, at peace to the first, and a tier within reach wins over the strategy). Mutation: 4 mutants, all caught.

## Part 4: missionaries by pressure, population and strategy

- **Where missionaries go** (10, religion layer: "faith spend on missionaries/apostles toward cities with low pressure and high population"). Before, a religious unit went to the nearest city not following its religion (ours 6 plots nearer). Now each citizen counts a plot nearer, and a city already holding our religion's share of the pressure counts up to 6 plots farther (`kMissionaryPressureWeight`), so missionaries go where passive pressure will not do the work.
- **Faith spent on them under a Religious strategy** (same line, "if Religious strategy active"). Before, any AI with a religion kept up to 3 religious units bought; now 3 under a Religious strategy and 1 otherwise, leaving Faith for worship buildings, Naturalists and Rock Bands.
- Checks: pace within noise (128 paired seeds; science -0.1 ± 0.4, gold -2.7 ± 4.2). Tests `ai_missionaries_seek_large_cities_with_little_of_their_religion` and `ai_buys_more_missionaries_under_a_religious_strategy`. Mutation: 6 mutants, 5 caught and 1 failing the build (dropping the pressure term leaves its share unused). Soak games: no crash, replay mismatch or reload drift.

## Part 5: promotions by value

- **The promotion worth most, not the first offered** (10: promotions weighed through the UnitPromotionClasses lists). Each offered promotion is tried on a copy of the game: the combat strength it adds against the foreign military units in our sight within 8 plots (`kPromotionFoeRange`), attacking and defending against how each would strike, those at war with us counting three times; then +12 a point of movement, +10 of range, +15 an extra attack and +3 of sight. Ties keep the order offered; the leader's promotions keep their own path.
- Checks: pace and speed unchanged (128 paired seeds; science +0.3 ± 0.7; the benchmark measures growth, not fights). Tests `ai_promotes_for_the_foes_around` (Tortoise against Archers, Battlecry against Warriors; foes at war, out of range and out of sight) and `ai_promotes_a_ship_for_speed_with_no_foes_near` (Helmsman alone, Embolon beside an enemy Galley). Mutation: 6 mutants, all caught. Soak games: no crash, replay mismatch or reload drift.
