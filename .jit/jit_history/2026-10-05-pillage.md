# Plan: pillage and repair (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-05. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-05-competitions.md` (and an AI economy pass recorded in `jit_history/2026-10-05-engineers.md`). Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent: units pillaging improvements and districts, a core war mechanic of 05-units-and-combat the core lacked entirely. One milestone, one PR.

Specs: 05-units-and-combat (War support systems: Pillage); data/districts (Pillage column); data/global-parameters (PILLAGE_MOVEMENT_COST).

## Milestone

1. **Done: pillage and repair:** `Pillage` (command 55) and `RepairImprovement` (command 56); plunder from the data; pillaged improvements yield nothing until a Builder repairs them; pillaged districts lose their adjacency and their buildings idle until they recover. AI and barbarians pillage; AI Builders repair first; the Unreal build chooser offers both.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Who and where: a military land unit with moves, on a plot owned by a civ it is at war with (barbarians are at war with all), not a city center, with an unpillaged improvement or a completed district. It costs PILLAGE_MOVEMENT_COST (3) moves (all that is left when fewer).
- Plunder (generated `plunder` on districts from the data's Pillage column; on improvements from 05's examples: Farm heals 50 HP, Mine 50 Gold, Plantation, Pasture, Quarry and Camp 25 Faith, every other improvement 25 Gold, Sovereign's reading): Gold and Faith to the treasury, Science to the current research, Culture to the current civic, Heal to the unit.
- A pillaged improvement keeps `pillagedTurns` 255 (the disaster countdown, at its longest) until a Builder on it uses `RepairImprovement`: the Builder's turn, no charge. A pillaged district (`CityDistrict::pillagedTurns`, save version 44) recovers over 10 of its owner's turns (Sovereign reading; Civ repairs it with production) and meanwhile has no adjacency and its buildings give no yields, housing or amenities.
- AI: after its moves and attacks, every unit that can pillage does; Builders repair a pillaged improvement they stand on first and value walking to one (70). Barbarians pillage the plot they stand on before acting.
- Coastal raids (added after): `Command::coastalRaid` is a `Pillage` with arg 1 and a target, for a naval melee unit or raider next to an enemy land plot with something to pillage; same plunder and cost. The AI's ships and barbarian ships raid the coast beside them.
- Not modelled: pillaging routes, the advanced pillage cost (1 move) for promoted units, pillaged buildings repaired by production (PILLAGE_BUILDING_REPAIR_PERCENT), the Drake's Legacy and Thunderbolt plunder bonuses.
- Fix found on the way: the Great Work Heist test named `DISTRICT_THEATER` (the id is `DISTRICT_THEATER_SQUARE`), so its district had type -1, which `districtAdjacency` then indexed; it surfaced as a crash once `CityDistrict` grew.
