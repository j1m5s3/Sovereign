# Plan: diplomacy depth: war weariness, alliances, emergencies (step 6, "everything else")

Status: done, 2026-10-05. Previous: `jit_history/2026-10-05-late-game.md`. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent: the Civ VI diplomacy systems the specs describe and the core lacks (James's leader decision keeps "war penalties as in Civ: war weariness, grievances, amenities"). James can redirect at any point.

Specs: 08-diplomacy-city-states-governors (Diplomatic actions: Alliance and the alliance levels table; War: war weariness; Emergencies [R&F]); data/global-parameters (WAR_WEARINESS_*, ALLIANCE_*, DIPLOMACY_ALLIANCE_TIME_LIMIT); data/world-congress-emergencies (emergencies and their rewards).

## Milestones (one PR each)

1. **Done: War weariness:** points against each opponent from combat on foreign ground, units lost and nuclear weapons launched; decay at war and at peace, most of it lifted by peace; an amenity lost in every city per 400 points; Propaganda, Martial Law, Defense of the Motherland and Fascism scale it; grievances against the enemy soften it. AI makes peace sooner when weariness bites; the HUD shows it.
2. **Done: Alliances:** the five types (Research, Military, Economic, Cultural, Religious) between declared friends with Civil Service, 30 turns and renewable; alliance points and levels 1-3 with the level effects the core can carry; allies share war grievances (50%) and are called to arms. AI proposes and accepts alliances with civs it likes; the diplomacy screen offers them.
3. **Done: Emergencies:** Military (a city of another civ captured), City-State (a city-state captured), Religious (a Holy City converted), Nuclear (a nuclear weapon used) and Betrayal (war on a friend or ally) emergencies; eligible civs join, the target gets the opposite goal; success and failure rewards (favor, the target's rewards). AI joins when it shares the goal; the HUD lists them.

## Decisions (Claude's recommendations; James gave standing consent)

- Milestone 1 as built: `Player::warWeariness` holds points against each other player (save version 39). Combat on a plot not one's own adds WAR_WEARINESS_PER_COMBAT_IN_FOREIGN_LANDS (2) to that side (a city assault counts for the attacker), a unit lost WAR_WEARINESS_PER_UNIT_KILLED (3), a nuclear launch WAR_WEARINESS_PER_WMD_LAUNCHED (10) against each civ hit; never against barbarians. Gains scale by the new player modifier ADJUST_WAR_WEARINESS_PERCENT (Propaganda -25, Martial Law -25, Defense of the Motherland -100, Fascism +20) and drop 1% per 10 grievances held against the enemy, at most 50% (Sovereign's reading of "reduced by grievances"). As the player's turn begins each opponent's points decay by 50 at war or 200 at peace; peace takes 2000 at once. Every city loses an amenity per WAR_WEARINESS_POINTS_FOR_AMENITY_LOSS (400) total points. Combat in allied lands counts 1 (milestone 2); the WAR_WEARINESS_LOSS_OVER_REQ_AMENITIES_* parameters are not used. AI: two amenities lost to weariness make a war it is not clearly winning end sooner. Unreal: the HUD shows the points and the amenities lost.
- Milestone 2 as built: a deal item `Alliance` (amount = type: Research, Military, Economic, Cultural, Religious) between declared friends who both have Civil Service and are not allied; it runs DIPLOMACY_ALLIANCE_TIME_LIMIT (30) turns on both relations (`Relation::alliance`, `allianceUntil`, `alliancePoints`; save version 40) and lapses after. Points come as each side's turn begins: ALLIANCE_POINTS_MULTIPLIER (4) plus ALLIANCE_POINTS_FOR_TRADE (1) for a trade route each way; level 2 at ALLIANCE_LEVEL_TWO_XP (320), level 3 at ALLIANCE_LEVEL_THREE_XP (960). A new type starts the points over. Effects carried: level 1 routes to the ally carry +2 Science (Research), +4 Gold (Economic), +2 Culture (Cultural) or +2 Faith (Religious) for the origin; Military level 1 +5 strength against a civ the ally is also at war with; Military level 2 shared sight; Research and Cultural level 3 add 10% of the ally's city Science or Culture. Every alliance gives +1 Diplomatic Favor a turn (WORLD_CONGRESS_ALLIANCE_FAVOR_PER_TURN); allies share 50% of a war declaration's grievances (SHARE_WAR_GRIEVANCES_ALLY); combat on an ally's land adds 1 war weariness instead of 2; war between allies ends the alliance. Not modelled at first: the destination's route yields, research agreements, the +15% production, free promotion XP, envoy and suzerain sharing, loyalty and religious pressure exemptions, the religious strength and pressure, tourism sharing, the Faith per follower. All but research agreements, suzerain sharing and the +20 pressure followed in `2026-10-06-alliance-effects.md`. AI: offers an alliance to a declared friend it likes (opinion 15+, the type its strategy wants: Research for Science, Military for Domination or at war, Cultural, Religious, else Economic), accepts on the same bar, and answers a call to arms: it joins the war of an ally that was declared on, against a civ it is not friends with. Unreal: the HUD lists alliances with their level and turns left; the diplomacy screen offers them through the deal items.
- Milestone 3 as built (`src/emergencies.cpp`; `GameState::emergencies`, save version 41): a civ becomes an emergency's target when it takes a major civ's city (Military) or a city-state (City-State) in `captureCity`, when another religion's Holy City comes to follow its religion (Religious, checked each world turn, once per Holy City and converter), when it launches a nuclear weapon (Nuclear), or when it declares war on a declared friend or ally (Betrayal). One running emergency of a kind per target. The civ wronged joins at once; any living major civ that has met the target and is not its friend or ally may join with `JoinEmergency` (command 52) on its own turn while it runs. Goals: Military and City-State, the city no longer held by the target; Religious, the Holy City following its own religion; Nuclear and Betrayal, a member taking any city of the target. Military, City-State and Religious run 30 turns, Nuclear and Betrayal 60. Success: each member +100 Diplomatic Favor; failure at the end: the target +200. Members declare war on the target without grievances (an Emergency War); a running Betrayal emergency adds 50% to the target's war weariness against members. Sovereign change: a declared friend or ally may now be attacked (it was forbidden), which is what makes a Betrayal. Not modelled: the combat bonuses and penalties, healing, relics, the religious pressure burst, the Gold per envoy, the special session and its favor costs, competitions (Nobel prizes, World's Fair, World Games, Climate Accords, International Space Station) and aid requests. AI: joins an emergency against a civ it dislikes or that outmatches it, goes to war on an emergency's target at three quarters of its usual margin, and never betrays a friend or ally itself. Unreal: the HUD lists running emergencies; the , (World Congress) chooser offers joining.
