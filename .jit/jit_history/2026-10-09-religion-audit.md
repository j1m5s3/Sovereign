# Record: religion rules the spec audit found off (06)

Status: done, 2026-10-09. Previous: `2026-10-09-volcanoes.md`. A read-only audit compared specs/civ6/06-religion.md with the core, leaving out what `2026-10-06-spec-audit-part-2.md`, `2026-10-06-belief-effects.md` and the decisions already settle, and confirmed 6 rules that differ in code. Chosen by Claude under James's standing consent; one PR.

## Change

- **Trade routes carry pressure both ways** (06: "the origin's religion is pushed into the destination at factor 1.0, and the destination's into the origin at 0.5"; data: `RELIGION_SPREAD_TRADE_ROUTE_PRESSURE_FOR_DESTINATION`, `_FOR_ORIGIN`). Before, only the origin's religion travelled. Each turn a route runs, the destination gains the origin's majority religion at the base passive pressure times 1.0, and the origin gains the destination's times 0.5; a fraction is counted over the route's turns, so half a point a turn is a point every other turn. Both majorities are read before either city gains (`Game::processTrade`). The amount per turn is still the base pressure, as the spec leaves it unverified.
- **A Great Prophet founds a religion on its civ's Stonehenge** (06: "activated on a completed Holy Site ... or on Stonehenge"); the city holding it becomes the Holy City. Before, only a Holy Site would do. The AI's Prophet heads for the nearer of its Holy Sites and its Stonehenge (`Game::canFoundReligion`; `prophet` in `src/ai.cpp`).
- **Holy Order makes Missionaries and Apostles cheaper, and nothing else** (06; data: one modifier for each). Before, its 30% came off every unit bought with Faith: Gurus, Inquisitors, Warrior Monks, Naturalists and Rock Bands too (`Game::faithPurchaseCost`).
- **Monastic Isolation spares its own religion** (06: "your religion's pressure never drops from theological combat losses"). Before, it was read from the losing unit's owner, so a unit of another religion owned by a player with the belief lost nothing, and a unit of the belief's religion owned by someone else lost pressure. Now the losing unit's religion decides (`Game::theologicalCombat`).
- **Religious Unity pays once per city-state, however it turned** (06: "+1 envoy when a city-state first adopts your religion"). Before, the envoy came only when the turn's passive pressure tipped the city-state, so a Missionary's conversion, a trade route or a theological win paid nothing, and a city-state turning away and back paid again. Now each turn the founder gains an envoy for each city-state following its religion that has not done so before, kept in `Player::unityCityStates` (save version 89; under Rogue State the city-state counts but no envoy comes) (`Game::processReligion`).
- **Monumentality in a Golden Age buys civilian land units with Faith** (06: Faith is spent on "builders/settlers with the Monumentality dedication"; data: "can buy Landcivilian units with Faith ... while player has golden age"). Builders, Settlers, Traders and Archaeologists cost their Gold price in Faith (Builders and Settlers 30% off, as before) and are finished as if bought with Gold: a Builder's charges, a Settler's population. A unit bought with Faith now needs the city's population as with Gold (`Game::faithPurchaseCost`, the Purchase command).

Results:
- 128 AI games (Small, 6 AI, turn 200) against main: no change worth the name (science -0.1 ± 0.1, culture -0.1 ± 0.1, Gold +0.7 ± 1.6). Eight 150-turn 6-AI games take about 2% longer (11.9 s against 11.6 s, three runs each); they play out differently.
- 8 long AI games in 8 setups (up to Huge, 400 turns): no crash or replay mismatch; all 8 play out differently.
- Tests: `trade_routes_carry_pressure_both_ways` (four turns of a route, both directions), `a_great_prophet_founds_a_religion_on_stonehenge` (finished, unfinished, another civ's), `holy_order_discounts_missionaries_and_apostles_only` (Missionary, Apostle; Guru, Inquisitor, Naturalist), `monastic_isolation_spares_its_religion_whoever_owns_the_unit`, `religious_unity_pays_once_per_city_state` (a city-state already turned, then away and back, through a save), `monumentality_buys_civilians_with_faith` (a Builder's charges, a Settler's population and price, a Warrior refused, the population needed); `lahores_suzerain_buys_nihangs_that_military_buildings_strengthen` now expects a Naturalist at full price under Holy Order. Mutation check: 17 mutants, all caught.

## Unsure, not changed

- Whether a city presses itself with its own majority religion (the core says no).
- Whether new citizens add pressure to the majority religion (the epochalmanac reading) or unbelief presses 50 per citizen (the data's name, `RELIGION_SPREAD_ATHEISM_PRESSURE_PER_POP`, which the core follows).
- Whether a religion can be founded without a pantheon (the core allows it; the spec does not say).
- How much pressure a trade route carries per turn (the core uses the base passive pressure, without the Holy Site and Holy City multipliers).
