## Reading rules
- **Precedence:** `specs/sovereign/` overrides `specs/civ6/` where they differ. Civ VI is the baseline Sovereign clones; Sovereign docs record the deliberate changes.
- **Prose before data:** the numbered `specs/civ6/NN-*.md` files explain rules (3-9k tokens each). `specs/civ6/data/*.md` are generated lookup tables; never read one whole.
- **Look up, don't load:** in a data file, `grep -n '^## '` for its sections, then `grep -n '<name>'` for the row you need (the header row is the first `|` line under the section heading). Big ones: civilizations-leaders (~25k tok), units (~13k), great-people (~12k), governments-policies (~9k).
- **Generated files:** `specs/civ6/data/*.md` come from `tools/civ6_extract`; change the extractor, never the tables.
- **Unverified rules** are tagged `(engine; unverified)` in the prose; grep for it before relying on an engine number.

## Subsystems
- [core/] — the C++ rules core (no Unreal dependency): fixed-point, RNG streams, hex grid, rules loader, game state, commands, map gen, fog of war, movement, cities (`src/city.cpp`), research, boosts, governments and policies (`src/research.cpp`), builders, improvements, luxuries and strategics (`src/improvements.cpp`), naval movement, embarkation and the Harbor (`src/game.cpp` movement, `src/combat.cpp`, `src/districts.cpp`), great people and Great Works (`src/greatpeople.cpp`, `data/rules/greatpeople.json`), trade routes and roads (`src/trade.cpp`), city-states and envoys (`src/citystates.cpp`, `data/rules/citystates.json`), grievances, Diplomatic Favor and the World Congress with the Diplomatic Victory (`src/worldcongress.cpp`, `data/rules/worldcongress.json`), espionage: spies beside the leader's assassins, operations and the 3d6 ladder (`src/espionage.cpp`, `data/rules/espionage.json`), governors: titles, appointment, promotion trees, establishing, loyalty and promotion effects (`src/governors.cpp`, `data/rules/governors.json`, GOVERNOR_PROMOTION_* modifiers), diplomacy: opinions with reasons and fading memories, the twelve leader agendas (`agenda` in `data/rules/civilizations.json`), deals, denouncing, friendship, open borders, peace deals and AI proposals (`src/diplomacy.cpp`; AI side in `src/ai.cpp` `deals`), eras, ages, historic moments, tourism and the culture victory (`src/eras.cpp`, `data/rules/moments.json`), world wonders (`src/wonders.cpp`, `data/rules/wonders.json`; wonders are buildings with a `wonder` flag), religion: pantheons, founding, beliefs, pressure, religious units, theological combat, religious victory (`src/religion.cpp`, `data/rules/religion.json`, belief modifiers in `data/rules/modifiers.json`), war and peace, unit and city combat, walls, capture, raze, elimination, ZOC, XP, promotions, upgrades and healing (`src/combat.cpp`), barbarian camps and raiders (`src/barbarians.cpp`), district placement, cost, adjacency, appeal and district housing/amenities (`src/districts.cpp`), the playable leader: gear, escorts, capture, succession, regicide, assassins, the aura, citizen stances, reputation and rebellion (`src/leader.cpp`), loyalty and the Free Cities (`src/loyalty.cpp`), the live battle contract (`src/combat.cpp`), the AI player (`src/ai.cpp`, `include/sovereign/ai.h`; pace benchmark `sovsim --bench`, `tests/test_ai_pace.cpp`), score and victories (`src/victory.cpp`), modifiers (`src/modifiers.cpp`), saves. `core/README.md` has build/test commands and a file map.
- [battle/] — the live battle simulation (squads, orders, flanking, bracing, the leader's aura) and the trained battle AI: commander interface, a small network with its own forward pass, the self-play trainer `battle_train`, tests. Plain C++17 without Unreal or the core; floats allowed (a live battle is an input to the core). `battle/README.md` has build, training and design notes.
- [diplomacy/] — the dialogue layer for language-model diplomacy: persona and prompts from the rules (agenda, voice, relationship, opinion reasons, past talks), parsing a model's structured reading into deal items, the scripted fallback model, input and output safety filters, the llama.cpp server client behind a `Transport` interface, `Conversation` (the rules decide, the model only speaks), the `diplo_chat` tool, tests. Plain C++17 over the core; the socket client is a separate library kept out of Unreal. `diplomacy/README.md` has the file map and how to run a model.
- [net/] — online play: the lockstep session over the core's command log (`Host` orders commands and plays AI seats, `Client` applies the stream, per-turn state hashes with save-based resync, seat claims, drops and rejoins, chat), the `Link`/`Listener` transport interface with loopback and TCP (`sovereign_net_tcp`, kept out of Unreal), the `sovnet` tool, tests. `net/README.md` has the design and how to run a game across processes.
- [unreal/] — UE 5.8 project (`Sovereign.uproject`). `SovereignCore` compiles `core/src`, `battle/src`, `diplomacy/src` and `net/src` (not their socket code) through generated wrappers; the diplomacy screen is `SovDiplomacy*` (talk driver, engine HTTP transport, Slate panel); online play and hot seat go through `SovSession` (local, host and join modes) with engine-socket links in `SovNetLink` and Steam lobbies, invites and links in `SovSteam`; the main menu is in `SovPlayerController` (`OpenMenu`); `SovereignBridge` holds the session/subsystem that owns the `sov::Game`, the mirror of seat 0's knowledge (`SovMirror`), the hex map actor, input as commands (`SovPlayerController`), the canvas HUD, the City Center street scene (`SovStreetLayout`, `SovStreetScene`) and the live battle scene (`SovBattleSim`, an adapter over `battle/`, and `SovBattleScene`). `unreal/README.md` has build, run, controls and the headless automation tests.
- [core/include/sovereign/api.h] — `SOV_API` export marker for public core declarations (empty standalone; dllexport/import when Unreal loads the core as a DLL).
- [tools/check_unreal_core_module.py] — CTest check (and `--write` generator) keeping the Unreal core wrappers in step with `core/CMakeLists.txt`.
- [tools/art/] — scripted art pipeline: Blender Python generates kit pieces (Nature, Classical City Center, Figures), `ue_import.py` imports them into `unreal/Content/Art/` (committed), `build_art.py` runs both; `tools/art/README.md` has conventions. The game loads them through `SovArt` and falls back to primitives.
- [data/rules/] — rules data the core loads. `globals/terrain/resources/units/buildings/techs/civics/governments/policies/improvements.json` are generated by `tools/rules_gen/gen_rules.py` from `specs/civ6/data` (never edit by hand); `civilizations.json`, `leader.json` (leader unit, gear, dynasties, SOVEREIGN promotions, assassin unit and odds), `setup.json` and `modifiers.json` are hand-written Sovereign data.
- [tools/check_core_rules.py] — CTest check that keeps floats, nondeterministic RNG, unordered containers, wall clock and Unreal headers and macros out of `core/`.
- [.github/workflows/core.yml] — CI: build and test the core on Linux GCC, Linux Clang and Windows MSVC, plus a 100-turn replay check.
- [tools/civ6_extract/] — rebuilds the rules DB from a local Civ VI install and regenerates `specs/civ6/data/` (README.md there for usage; rerun after game patches).

## Decisions
- Clone Civ VI first (base + Rise and Fall + Gathering Storm), then layer the Sovereign twist (playable leader character).
- Engine: UE5 front end, separate C++ rules core with no Unreal dependency.
- Rules are data: load values from data files, never hard-code.
- Leader roster: historical figures only, nobody living or recently dead.
- Core foundations (engine doc): fixed-point math, Civ's modifier system, one command log, non-deterministic parts enter only as recorded commands, versioned saves.
- Live battles (leader doc §9): only melee involving the leader's stack goes live; core computes the Civ result and the scene shifts it within a band; one machine hosts each scene; the leader fights personally in every era.
- Leader ability belongs to the throne; every successor keeps it (leader doc §5).
- Battles in every era use one design (era tech only differs); air attacks stay Civ math.
- Online: Steamworks first, Epic Online Services too.
- The 3D world is generated from what the viewing player knows, not the true state (world doc).
- Core implementation choices (fixed-point scale, RNG, hex layout, command log, save format, rules layering): see Decisions in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-rules-core.md`.
- Leader choices (own 1UPT layer, gear ladder and balance rule, escort-first defence, capture vs kill, interregnum, successor pool with a regent stand-in, Regicide, assassins as off-map agents and their odds, one-branch promotions, aura): see Decisions and Answers in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-leader-classic.md`.
- Street and battle choices (loyalty formula and Free Cities as a lazily created barbarian-flagged player, stance numbers, reputation thresholds, rebellion, City Center generator, PendingBattle contract with a ±25% band, scripted battle simulation): see Decisions in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-street-and-battle.md`.
- Remaining Civ systems choices (dependency order, generated data with typed effects and the rest kept as text, wonders as buildings, city-states as players, a temporary era-score threshold adjustment): see Decisions and the built notes in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-civ-systems.md`.
- Governors, espionage, World Congress and climate choices (governor effects through the modifier model, spies in the agent pool and the 3d6 ladder, grievance bases and favor, the resolution subset, CO2 and climate points, hashed coastal lowlands, disaster odds, sites and damage, what is left out): see Decisions in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-civ-systems-2.md`.
- Strategy AI and difficulty choices (measure with `sovsim --bench`, the housing districts added on the way, stateless per-turn strategies and posture, staged operations with Civ's strength ratios, upgrade cost formula, skill-first difficulty with AI bonuses only at Immortal and Deity): see Decisions in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-strategy-ai.md`.
- Battle AI choices (headless `battle/` library shared by Unreal, the trainer and tests; three squads and six orders a second; 49-input network with its own forward pass behind `Commander`; evolution strategies by self-play; ship the version with the best worst case against scripted styles; scripted fallback when no weights): see Decisions in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-battle-ai.md`.
- Art choices (code as the source of truth, vertex-painted flat shading, one kit material with a team-colour slot, committed generated assets, kits by recipe): see Decisions in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-art-slice.md`.
- Unreal bridge choices (two modules, wrapper sources, `SOV_API`, C++-only project on the Entry map, mirror of seat 0's knowledge, polled input, AI seats stepped per tick): see Decisions in `.jit/jit_history/2026-10-05-unreal-bridge.md`.
